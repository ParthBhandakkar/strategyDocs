Overview

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Daily Bias

Timeframe: Daily.

Process: Look at the last two daily candles and identify their highs and lows.

Rules:

If the second candle closes above the high of the first candle, the bias for the third candle is bullish.

If the second candle wicks above the first candle's high but closes below it, the bias for the third candle is bearish.

Step 2: Identify Manipulation and Expansion

Timeframe: 15-minute.

Process: Mark the opening price of the third day. Wait for a manipulation move (a wick) to the opposite side of the expected direction that sweeps significant liquidity (e.g., significant highs/lows or relative equal highs/lows).

Tool: A Fibonacci tool can be used from the last high to the last low before the market structure shift to identify valid manipulation, which usually ends around -2 and -2.5 standard deviations.

Step 3: Execution

Process: Once a valid manipulation occurs, look for a market structure shift or a break-of-structure entry from a fair value gap.

Entry: Enter at the fair value gap.

Risk Management: Target a 1:2 to 1:3 reward-to-risk ratio.

Key Rules & Conditions

Identify the daily bias before trading.

Ensure the manipulation move sweeps significant liquidity on the 15-minute timeframe.

Not every manipulation move will lead to a valid expansion.

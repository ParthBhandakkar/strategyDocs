ICT Daily Bias Guide

Source: Faiz SMC ("I Simplified ICT Daily Bias..")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rf2Q46dQcSk

Overview

This video serves as a comprehensive guide to identifying daily bias using four distinct methods, ranging from mechanical to discretionary. The focus is on predicting whether the daily candle will expand bullishly or bearishly.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Method 1: Candle Closure Bias (Daily TF)

Logic: Based on the relationship between the first and second daily candles.

Rule: If the second candle closes below the low of the first, the third day is likely bearish. If it closes above the high of the first, it is likely bullish.

Trade: Look for a Judas Swing after 9:30 AM open and trade in the direction of the identified bias.

Method 2: Swing High/Low Draw on Liquidity (Daily TF)

Logic: Use swing highs/lows formed by 3 daily candles.

Rule: If a swing low forms, the high of the third candle is the target for the fourth day. If a swing high forms, the low of the third candle is the target for the fourth day.

Method 3: Discretionary Bias

Points: 1. What has price done? (Liquidity sweep? Tap into FVG/OB?) 2. Where is it coming from? 3. Where can it go? (Highs/lows, FVGs).

Goal: Move from external liquidity (highs/lows) to internal liquidity (FVGs).

Method 4: External to Internal Liquidity & FVG Inversion

Logic: Pair the sweep of external liquidity with the inversion of internal liquidity (FVGs).

Rule: Daily bias is confirmed by a 1h market structure shift. Switch bias to bullish/bearish only after all FVGs in a dealing range are inversed.

Key Rules & Conditions

Bias is a prediction of expansion.

Discretionary method relies on experience and market logic.

Inversion of FVGs on the daily/4h TF provides high probability bias confirmations.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Daily bias is about predicting the expansion move of a candle.

Use simple mechanical methods (Candle Closure/Swing High-Low) for high accuracy.

Use discretionary and structural methods (External to Internal) for more refined analysis.

Understand that price moves from liquidity (highs/lows) to internal levels (FVGs) and vice-versa.

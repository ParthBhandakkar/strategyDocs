NQ Funded Account Trading Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("This Simple NQ Trading Strategy Got Me Funded With $300,000")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rk3Lz5oS8pE

Overview

This video outlines a trading strategy for NQ (Nasdaq futures) used to successfully pass funded trading challenges. It combines higher time frame analysis with specific 1-minute entry triggers.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)

Higher Time Frame Analysis: Mark a Fair Value Gap on the 1-hour, 15-minute, or 5-minute timeframe.

Setup:

Wait for price to tap into the marked Fair Value Gap after or before the market open (9:30 AM New York time).

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe and look for SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence with ES (S&P 500 futures). NQ should create a new low/high that ES fails to mirror.

Define a dealing range (high to low) on the 1-minute timeframe.

Look for the inversion of a 1-minute Fair Value Gap within that dealing range.

Enter immediately upon the inversion (no waiting for a retest).

Set a stop-loss just below the bodies of the candles.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only trade NQ.

If the closest liquidity pool is hit before an entry is provided, the setup is invalid.

If there are multiple Fair Value Gaps in the dealing range, wait for price to inverse all of them.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (Passing 300k account):

Asset/Pair: NQ

Entry: Long after NQ made a lower low with SMT divergence on ES and inverted a 1-minute FVG.

Stop Loss: Below candle bodies.

Outcome: Hit target, account passed.

Risk Management

Aim for a 1:1.5 or 1:2 risk-reward ratio.

Go break-even at the closest liquidity pool.

Take a partial at 1:1.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Combine Higher Time Frame FVG with 1-minute SMT divergence.

Look for "V-shaped" instant recoveries after liquidity sweeps.

Immediate entry upon FVG inversion, no retest required.

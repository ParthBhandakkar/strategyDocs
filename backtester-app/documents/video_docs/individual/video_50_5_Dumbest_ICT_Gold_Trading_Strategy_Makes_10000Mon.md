Overview

This strategy focuses specifically on the Asian session (20:00 - 00:00 NY time). It emphasizes using higher timeframe (1-hour) PDAs (Fair Value Gaps/Order Blocks) and a specific entry model based on 5-minute inversion.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Bias Identification

Determine the 1-hour order flow. If bullish, only take longs. If bearish, only shorts.

Step 2: Time & PD Array

Trade during Asia (20:00 - 00:00 NY).

Wait for price to tap into a 1-hour PDA (FVG/OB).

Step 3: Execution

Drop to 5-minute timeframe. Wait for liquidity sweep.

Identify 5-minute FVG within the dealing range. Wait for inversion.

Enter on inversion.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade only during the specified Asia time window.

Stop loss below candle bodies.

If price hits the liquidity level before entry, setup is invalid.

Time and price must align (Asia session + 1-hour PDA).

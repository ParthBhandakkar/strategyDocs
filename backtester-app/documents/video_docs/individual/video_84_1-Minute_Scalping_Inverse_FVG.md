Strategy/Topic Name: 1-Minute Scalping (Inverse FVG)

Source: Faiz SMC ("Easy ICT 1 Minute Timeframe Trading Strategy That Works! (High Winrate)")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g7MxWxQ99_4

Overview

A detailed 5-step framework for 1-minute trading: Bias, Lower Timeframe Draw on Liquidity (DOL), Opposite Liquidity Sweep, Entry Model (Inverse FVG), and Risk Management.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Bias (Daily/4H/1H/15M)

Use the external-to-internal liquidity method.

Check for equal highs or equal lows on the highest timeframe.

If equal highs were swept, look for equal lows as the target.

Step 2: Setup (1-Minute)

Post-9:30 AM, look for an opposite-direction sweep.

If bearish, wait for a buy-side sweep. If bullish, wait for a sell-side sweep.

Step 3: Entry (Inverse FVG)

Look for all FVGs in a leg to be "inversed" (price closing through them).

Enter on the closure or retest of the inversed FVG.

Key Rules & Conditions

If price sweeps closest liquidity after entry, go to break-even immediately.

If the setup involves multiple FVGs, all must be inversed.

Setup is invalid if price sweeps the nearest liquidity before the setup completes.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Setup: 1-minute sweep of equal lows post-9:30 AM.

Entry: Inversed bullish FVG.

Target: Equal highs.

Risk Management

1:1 risk-to-reward ratio: Close 40% of the position.

Hold remaining 60% until the DOL (draw on liquidity) is hit.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Bias is king: The setup is meaningless without a correct bias.

The "Three Strike Pattern": Wait for the actual sweep post-9:30 AM, avoiding pre-open fake-outs.

Inverse FVGs are the primary entry triggers.

Overview

This strategy utilizes a 5-step framework based on the daily timeframe for bias and the 15-minute timeframe for entries. The core focus is on liquidity sweeps following the daily candle open.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Bias (Daily Timeframe)

Use three candles to determine bias.

Bullish Bias: If the second day candle closes above the first day's high.

Bearish Bias: If the second day candle closes below the first day's low.

Step 2: 15-Minute Setup

Once bias is established, drop to the 15-minute timeframe on the third day.

Mark the daily opening price.

Step 3: Liquidity Sweep & Entry

Bullish Bias: Wait for price to sweep sell-side liquidity (to the left of the opening price), look for an MSS, and enter at an order block or FVG.

Bearish Bias: Wait for price to sweep buy-side liquidity (to the left of the opening price), look for an MSS, and enter.

Key Rules & Conditions

If the body does not close above or below the previous candle in the 3-candle pattern, avoid trading that day.

Focus on price action after the 9:30 AM New York open for the highest probability distributions.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Bias: Bullish (2nd day closed above 1st day's high)

Setup: 15-minute sweep of sell-side liquidity below opening price, followed by MSS and FVG.

Outcome: Successful expansion to the upside.

Example 2:

Bias: Bearish (failed to close above high, rejection)

Setup: 15-minute sweep of buy-side liquidity (equal highs), followed by MSS.

Outcome: Successful trade to the downside, targeting inefficiency.

Risk Management

Goal is a 1:2 or 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio.

Keep trades short-term, typically aiming to be out within the day.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Use a 3-candle pattern to identify daily bias.

Trade in the direction of the bias on the third day.

Use 15-minute liquidity sweeps to enter the market.

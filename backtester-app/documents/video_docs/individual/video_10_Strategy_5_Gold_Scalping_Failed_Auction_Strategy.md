Strategy 5: Gold Scalping Failed Auction Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("ICT Not Working? Use This Gold Trading Strategy Instead..")

Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HyJuZNo_ikM

Overview

This strategy provides a simplified profile built specifically for Gold (XAU/USD). By using a dedicated 5-minute volume profile window, this mechanical strategy tracks session value distributions to catch reliable intraday reversals.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Chart & Timeframe Setup

Open your charting layout and set the timeframe strictly to 5-Minutes.

Configure your platform's time zone to New York Time.

Select the Fixed Range Volume Profile (FRVP) tracking tool.

Step 2: Map the Profile Reference Window

Identify the exact candle starting at 3:00 AM New York Time and draw your profile across to the close of the 7:00 AM New York Time candle.

This precise data window captures the crucial London session volume distribution, setting up clear trading parameters for the morning session.

Mark the resulting three key horizontal levels: Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and the Point of Control (POC) midline.

Step 3: Monitor Post-7 AM Value Breaks

Track price action carefully after the 7:00 AM profile window completes:

Scenario A: If price trades inside the Value Area at 7:00 AM, wait for it to break out and close completely outside the VAH or VAL boundary lines.

Scenario B: If price has already broken out and is trading outside the Value Area at 7:00 AM, monitor that location as your primary setup area.

Step 4: Execute on the Failed Auction Close

Wait for a 5-minute candle to confirm a clean close back inside the Value Area thresholds.

For short setups, price must break above VAH and then close back below it. For long setups, price must cross below VAL and then close back above it.

Enter a market position immediately on the inner close or on a brief retest of the value line. Place your protective stop loss orders directly beyond the manipulation wick high or low point.

Key Rules & Conditions

Strict Pair Focus: This strategy is optimized for Gold (XAU/USD or GC Futures). Stick to a single version of the asset to maintain consistent data tracking.

Execution Limits: Take a maximum of two trade setups per day, ensuring you only take one trade in each direction (one long and one short). Never take consecutive trades in the same direction, as the probability of success drops sharply.

No Extra Analysis: This approach is fully mechanical and self-contained; you do not need higher timeframe trend analysis to find valid entries.

Risk Management

Always move your open trade risk to break-even as soon as price reaches the POC midline. The POC acts as a major volume magnet where price can often consolidate or reverse unexpectedly.

If a candle closes too far away from the value line on the reversal move, manage your risk parameters carefully by waiting for a light retest before entering.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Tracking London session volume distribution from 3:00 AM to 7:00 AM provides clear, highly reliable trading ranges for Gold.

Failed Auction setups take advantage of the market's natural tendency to return to balance when prices are rejected outside of value.

Sticking to clear risk thresholds and taking quick partials at the POC ensures steady account growth.

Overview

This video breaks down the A+ Midas Model trade taken on a Friday. It reinforces the importance of identifying valid liquidity and using displacement to confirm entries.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)

Analysis: Identify the most recent unswept high and low (formed after 12:00 PM previous day) on the 15-minute timeframe.

The Trade: Wait for a sweep of the high or low at 8:00 PM. Drop to 1-minute timeframe for a market structure shift with displacement.

Inversion Entry: If the market structure shift is unclear or aggressive, wait for the inversion of the Fair Value Gaps formed within the dealing range to confirm the entry.

Key Rules & Conditions

Entry must be taken before the midnight open (12:00 AM).

Only two trades per session (8:00 PM and 9:00 PM, one trade each).

If you miss the 8:00 PM trade, you can look for the 9:00 PM trade.

Example 1 (Friday's Trade):

Asset/Pair: Gold

Risk Management

Aim for 1:2 risk-reward ratio.

Move to break-even at 1:1 risk-reward ratio.

Summary / Key Takeaways

If you lose multiple trades in a row, a single A+ trade (like this one, which yielded 11 RR) can significantly boost your overall profitability.

Success in trading is a long-term game; remain consistent with one model rather than chasing large daily percentages.

The 8 AM One-Candle Trading Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("This One Candle Can Change Your Life.. (Stupid Simple Strategy)")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YKbkZ4eRd04

Overview

This highly mechanical strategy strips away the need for traditional daily bias, directional over-analysis, or higher-timeframe macro trends. It relies entirely on a single structural reference block: the 1-Hour chart's 8:00 AM New York candle. The strategy exploits the systematic liquidity expansion that occurs immediately after this initial parameter is established.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Mark the 8:00 AM Reference Range

Open the 1-Hour (1H) chart and ensure your terminal clock is calibrated to New York time (EST). Locate the single candle that prints at 8:00 AM. Draw horizontal lines across the absolute high and the absolute low of this candle. This forms your trading range for the session.

Step 2: Monitor for a Premarket Liquidity Sweep

Wait for price action to expand outside of this 8:00 AM boundary line. The strategy requires the market to purposefully sweep either the 8:00 AM high line or the 8:00 AM low line, collecting buy or sell stops.

Step 3: Zoom In to the 1-Minute Execution Matrix

Once the high or low line is breached, drop down to the 1-Minute chart. Do not execute trades before 9:30 AM EST. If a liquidity sweep occurs prior to 9:30 AM, wait patiently for the New York equities open to print a secondary pool sweep or a confirmed structural market structure shift (MSS).

Step 4: Identify the Structural Shift and Range Re-entry

Look for a definitive 1-minute market structure shift (displacement breaking a swing high/low) pointing back toward the interior of the range. Critically, you must observe at least one 1-minute candle close cleanly back inside the original 8:00 AM high-to-low range boundaries.

Step 5: Execute via Premium or Discount PD Arrays

Once the market structure shift is confirmed inside the range boundaries, look for an active execution array left behind by the displacement leg. This can be a 1-minute breaker block, standard order block, or Fair Value Gap. Place your entry order directly at the mitigation level.

Step 6: Target Partial Profits and Final Draw

Set an initial take-profit order at a strict 1:2 risk-to-reward boundary. As soon as price reaches this level, take a 40% to 50% partial profit and immediately shift your stop loss to break-even. Leave the remaining portion of the trade to run to the opposite extreme of the 8:00 AM candle range.

Key Rules & Conditions

Asset Exclusion Rule: This strategy is designed exclusively for Nasdaq Futures (NQ). Do not trade it on other asset classes or indices.

Time Invalidation Rule: Trades must strictly be executed after the 9:30 AM New York bell has rung to avoid initial pre-market head-fakes.

The Close-In Rule: A market structure shift alone is insufficient. You must see price cross back over and print a body close inside the 8:00 AM range line to confirm institutional stop-hunting intent.

Risk Management

Rigorously adhere to the partial profit taking rule: lock in 40% to 50% of the trade at a 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.

Shift the stop loss to break-even immediately after the first profit target is hit. This creates a risk-free trade on the remaining position as it targets the opposite side of the range.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The 8:00 AM hourly candle high and low levels establish a reliable mechanical daily trading range.

Executions require a clear liquidity sweep outside this range, a market structure shift, and a candle body closing back inside the range.

Managing trades by securing partial profits at a 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio ensures consistent returns, even during high-volatility pullbacks.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Plot the 8:00 AM 1-Hour Candle Range

Set your charting time zone to New York local time. Allow the 8:00 AM 1-hour candle to close completely at 9:00 AM, then mark its exact high and low levels on your chart. This range forms the basis for your session setups.

Step 2: Track the 9:00 AM Candle Opening Price

Once the 9:00 AM 1-hour candle opens, drop down to the 1-minute chart. Watch for price to expand outward and sweep either the marked 8:00 AM high or low.

Step 3: Validate Lower Time Frame Shift and Range Return (1-Minute Chart)

Your entry setup requires two specific structural conditions on the 1-minute chart:

Market Structure Shift (MSS): A clear trend shift confirmed by a 1-minute candle body closing past the local swing high/low. Wicks do not count.

Range Return Close: The price action must close explicitly back inside the 8:00 AM candle range (below the high for shorts, or above the low for longs). This confirms a valid liquidity sweep rather than a breakout expansion.

Step 4: Execute on the Breaker Block Retest

Identify the 1-minute breaker block created during the sweep and structural shift sequence:

Set a limit order at the breaker block's boundary to enter on a retest.

Stop Loss Placement: Place your stop loss safely beyond the manipulation swing high/low, or just above the breaker block boundary if you prefer a tight, high-leverage risk profile.

Step 5: Map Your Profit Targets

Target the opposite end of the 8:00 AM 1-hour candle range (the 8:00 AM low for short trades, or the 8:00 AM high for long trades). This alignment can produce high risk-to-reward ratios, often reaching 1:5 to 1:7.

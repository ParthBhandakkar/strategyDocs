Overview

This strategy provides a highly structured, time-restricted mechanical trading model for gold (XAU/USD) during the Asian session open. It captures systemic expansions driven by algorithmic resets at a specific time, utilizing a 15-minute structural anchor and a 1-minute execution frame.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Synchronize Platform Time Zone Metrics

Set your charting platform time zone to New York local time (EST/EDT). This ensures precise alignment with the algorithmic windows utilized by the strategy.

Step 2: Plot Your Pre-Open Reference High & Low (15-Minute Chart)

At 7:59 PM New York time, look to the left of the upcoming 8:00 PM session open on the 15-minute chart:

Identify and mark the most recent, fully completed structural swing high and swing low.

These reference points must be formed after the previous day's New York session ended and must be fully completed before the 8:00 PM candle opens.

If a prominent high or low was already swept prior to 8:00 PM, look further left to the second most recent, unswept structural array.

Step 3: Monitor for the 8:00 PM Session Algorithm Sweep

As the Asian session opens at 8:00 PM, watch for price to expand and sweep either your pre-open reference high or low. Once a sweep occurs, drop down to the 1-minute chart to prepare for entry.

Step 4: Validate Lower Time Frame Shift and Range Return (1-Minute Chart)

Your entry setup requires two structural conditions on the 1-minute chart:

Market Structure Shift (MSS): A clear, clean structural shift confirmed by a 1-minute candle body closing past the local swing high/low. Wicks do not count.

Range Return Close: The market structure shift or a subsequent candle must close explicitly back inside your 15-minute trading range (below the reference high for shorts, or above the reference low for longs). This confirms a valid liquidity raid rather than a breakout.

Step 5: Select Your Entry Array

The Breaker Block Model (High Displacement): If the 1-minute chart prints a sharp displacement that travels far from the swept high/low, mark the local breaker block. Set a limit order at the breaker block's boundary for entry on a retest.

The Fair Value Gap Model (Moderate Displacement): If the displacement is moderate and stays close to the swept reference line, set a limit order at the extreme 1-minute Fair Value Gap (FVG) or order block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Asset Filter: This strategy is optimized specifically for gold (XAU/USD).

Time Macro Window: All entry setups must be executed within the 8:00 PM to 12:00 AM midnight New York time frame. If no trade triggers before 12:00 AM, cancel all pending orders and walk away.

Opposite Array Invalidation Rule: If price triggers a sweep of your reference high but sweeps the reference low before your 1-minute entry setup triggers, the trade is completely invalid. Do not enter.

Risk Management

The 1:1 Break-Even Rule: Once price hits a 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio, take a partial profit and move your stop loss to entry to ensure a risk-free position.

Psychology Management: The choice to manage trades with tight trailing stops or leave them to hit full targets depends on individual psychology and risk tolerance.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading a single position per day using strict algorithmic rules improves consistency and helps eliminate emotional decision-making.

Requiring price to close back inside the 15-minute range protects against trading expansion extensions and false turnarounds.

Setting your time zones correctly ensures accurate tracking of the daily session resets.

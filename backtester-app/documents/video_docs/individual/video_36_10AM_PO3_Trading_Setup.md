Overview

This video reveals a specific internal day-trading mechanism built entirely around the 4-hour candle power of three (PO3) model—specifically targeting the 10:00 AM New York time candle. This mechanical strategy is designed to isolate institutional accumulation, manipulation, and distribution (AMD) cycles within highly specific daily windows across NQ, Gold, and futures markets.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Establish Internal Day Bias via 4-Hour Timeframes

To execute a PO3 model on a 10:00 AM candle, you must accurately determine its overall trajectory beforehand using preceding market behavior. The directional bias is mechanical, tracking how the 6:00 AM 4-hour candle closes relative to the prior 2:00 AM 4-hour candle.

Timeframes Used: 4-hour (4H) chart.

Key Conditions to Confirm Trend Bias:

Bullish Bias: The 6:00 AM 4H candle must print a structural close fully above the absolute high of the 2:00 AM 4H candle.

Bearish Bias: The 6:00 AM 4H candle sweeps or wicks above the 2:00 AM high but fails to sustain momentum, explicitly closing below that 2:00 AM structural high.

Filter Rules (No Trade): If the 6:00 AM candle expands erratically and sweeps both the 2:00 AM high and low, no directional edge remains. Similarly, if it breaks neither side and remains entirely nested inside, avoid trading.

Step 2: Drop Down and Map Liquidity Pools at the Candle Open

As soon as the 10:00 AM 4H candle begins to form, transition to lower execution charts to locate structural validation points.

Timeframes Used: 5-minute (5M) chart.

Key Levels to Watch: Plot the exact opening price of the 10:00 AM candle. Look exclusively to the left side of this vertical opening axis to locate clear 5M structural highs or lows (internal liquidity pools) engineered prior to 10:00 AM.

Step 3: Wait for Liquidity Purge and Execution Confirmation

Allow the initial phase of the 10:00 AM candle to engineer its manipulation wick by running the targeted structural points identified in Step 2.

Timeframes Used: 5-minute (5M) chart.

Execution Triggers: Once the internal pool is swept, look for one of two specific micro-structural signatures:

Change in a State of Delivery (CISD): Formed when price closes through the body of the consecutive bearish or bullish candles responsible for running the liquidity.

Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG): The concrete failure and structural breakdown/inversion of the first opposite-facing 5M fair value gap printed during the immediate 10:00 AM leg.

Entry Method: Enter immediately at the market close of the validating 5M candle or position a limit order on a micro-retest of the structural CISD/IFVG signature.

Key Rules & Conditions

Asset Restrictions & Timing: NQ futures and general futures markets use the 10:00 AM New York time zone marker. Spot Gold (Forex pair XAUUSD) can also utilize this structural layout, but the operational candle shift occurs at 9:00 AM New York time.

Direction Correlation: You are strictly forbidden from searching for buys during a bearish narrative or sells during a bullish narrative. The 5M sweeps must align against the bias to trap retail counterparties.

Patience Filters: If a 10:00 AM candle initiates an immediate directional run straight into distribution without engineering an initial counter-trend manipulation wick to sweep left-side liquidity, the trade is voided.

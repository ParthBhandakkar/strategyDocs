Overview

This final strategy masterclass targets the structural inefficiencies of lower-timeframe candle ranges on Nasdaq Futures (NQ). By shifting execution focus from subjective order blocks to objective candle high/low sweeps and multi-timeframe Inversion FVGs, this strategy provides a highly mechanical trading framework.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Establish Macro Reference Boundaries (Daily & 1-Hour)

Begin your analysis by marking key liquidity levels on the Daily and 1-Hour charts. Identify major session highs, lows, and unmitigated Fair Value Gaps that are likely to act as structural magnets for price.

Step 2: Mark the Intraday 8:00 AM Range Anchor

On the 1-Hour (1H) chart, locate the candle that prints at 8:00 AM EST. Draw clear horizontal lines across its absolute high and absolute low points to establish your intraday range.

Step 3: Monitor for an Institutional Session Sweep

Wait for price action to expand outside this 8:00 AM range after the 9:30 AM New York market open. Look for an aggressive push that sweeps past the range high or range low line, hunting liquidity.

Step 4: Validate via Lower-Timeframe Inversions (5-Min to 1-Min)

Once the liquidity sweep occurs, drop down to the 5-Minute chart to identify the highest-probability execution level. Look for a counter-trend FVG to form, and wait for a candle body to close completely on the opposite side, creating an Inversion FVG (IFVG). If no clear setup forms on the 5-minute chart, check the 4-minute, 3-minute, and 2-minute charts sequentially down to the 1-minute chart.

Step 5: Execute Position at the Inversion Candle Close

Enter your trade immediately at the close of the first candle that prints a clear body close across the chosen FVG boundary line, transforming it into an active inversion array.

Step 6: Target Localized Liquidity Pools

Set your stop loss just past the physical candle bodies of the stop-hunt structure. Place your profit target order at high-probability levels, such as equal highs/lows or the opposite extreme of the 8:00 AM range line.

Key Rules & Conditions

The Asset Mandate Rule: This strategy is designed exclusively for Nasdaq Futures (NQ) and high-volume index charts. Do not apply it to slow-moving or low-liquidity assets.

The Inversion Hierarchy Law: Always prioritize the highest available timeframe inversion setup (from 5-minute down to 1-minute) to effectively filter out market noise.

The Pre-Entry Invalidation Rule: If price retraces and sweeps your target liquidity pool before a candle prints a valid inversion close on your execution chart, the trade setup is immediately voided.

Risk Management

Protect capital by moving your stop loss to break-even as soon as price clears the first localized counter-structure high or low post-entry.

Limit risk to a strict 1% max per position, and use physical candle body closes for structural invalidation limits rather than volatile wick extensions.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Tracking liquidity sweeps outside the 8:00 AM range provides a reliable mechanical framework for identifying intraday reversals.

Prioritizing higher-timeframe inversion arrays (up to the 5-minute chart) filters out lower-timeframe noise and improves win rates.

Managing trades by moving stops to break-even after initial structural breaks protects capital and ensures long-term consistency.

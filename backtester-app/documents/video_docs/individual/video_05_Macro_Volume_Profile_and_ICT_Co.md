Overview

This video reveals an advanced institutional day trading strategy that combines the macro structural levels of multiple Volume Profiles with the precise execution entry triggers of Inner Circle Trader (ICT) concepts. By mapping major value boundaries across multiple timeframes, traders can locate high-probability turning points and enter with minimal risk on a 1-minute chart.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Map the Four Core Volume Profiles

Core Principle: Trading success relies on combining precise key levels with robust execution confirmations. This strategy plots four distinct volume profiles to build a comprehensive map of institutional value:

Previous Week's Entire Volume Profile: Drawn from the weekly market open (Sunday/Monday at 18:00) to the weekly close (Friday at 16:55).

Previous Day's Volume Profile: Drawn from 18:00 to 16:55 of the immediately preceding trading session.

Overnight Volume Profile: Drawn from 18:00 of the prior evening until 09:30 AM New York time of the current tradable day.

New York Session Developing Volume Profile: Plotted from 09:30 AM onwards with the "extend" setting enabled to watch the value area build in real-time.

Key Level Rule: Only the levels (VAH, VAL, POC) from the Previous Week, Previous Day, and Overnight Profiles are treated as actionable key levels. The developing New York profile is used strictly to observe where current intraday volume is transacting.

Step 2: Identify Failed Auction and Breakout Conditions

Timeframe: Monitor key level interactions on the 5-minute timeframe.

Failed Auction Setup: Price wicks or briefly closes past a major macro boundary (e.g., previous day VAH or overnight VAL) but prints a 5-minute candle closing back inside the boundary, signaling institutional rejection.

Breakout Setup: Price breaks clear of a macro level and, instead of rejecting, holds a retest and sustains its position above/below the level before expanding with heavy displacement.

Step 3: Execute 1-Minute ICT Entry Confluences

Timeframe: Drop down to the 1-minute timeframe the exact moment a 5-minute retest of a validated macro level occurs.

Triggers: Look for a high-probability ICT pattern to execute the trade. Acceptable execution triggers include:

CISD (Change in State of Delivery)

IFVG (Inversion Fair Value Gap) - The author's highly preferred entry trigger

Market Structure Shift (MSS) accompanied by a classic Fair Value Gap (FVG)

Breaker Blocks or Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) configurations.

Key Rules & Conditions

The POC Magnet Rule: The Point of Control (POC) of any historical profile acts as a massive price magnet. Expect price to gravitate toward these levels and consolidate due to the high density of fair value transactions.

Multi-Profile Overlap: When key lines from different profiles overlap (e.g., a previous day POC aligning with an overnight VAL), the structural strength of that key level is significantly amplified.

Retest Verification: Never enter a breakout blindly on the first impulsive bar. Wait for price to establish a verified structural retest to confirm that the level has flipped from support to resistance (or vice versa).

Risk Management

The Large Wick Risk Mitigation Rule: When a macro turning point produces an execution setup with an excessively large 1-minute wick, a standard FVG entry can result in a massive, inefficient stop loss. To manage risk, do not enter blindly; instead, utilize an Optimal Trade Entry (OTE - 70.5% retracement) or a Rejection Block entry to keep the risk tightly controlled.

Target Partialing: Because macro profile levels act as significant institutional hurdles, always secure partial profits or move the stop loss to break-even when price interacts with major internal levels like the overnight POC or opposite value boundaries.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Volume profiles act as a reliable macro map, revealing exactly where institutional capital has historical interest, while ICT concepts serve as the precision micro trigger.

Tracking multiple profile horizons (weekly, daily, overnight) provides a layered look at support and resistance that single-horizon charts miss.

Discipline is essential; avoid over-trading by focusing exclusively on high-confluence zones where macro profile extremes overlap with clean 1-minute structural shifts.

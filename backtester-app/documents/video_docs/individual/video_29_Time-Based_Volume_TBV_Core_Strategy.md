Overview

This strategy breaks down a powerful, highly mechanical trading model known as Time-Based Volume (TBV). By tracking single-candle institutional absorption blocks contextually anchored to same-timeframe Fair Value Gaps, this strategy isolates high-probability reversals without requiring complex macro analysis.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Timeframe Allocation Boundaries

Lock in the core chart parameters required to maintain algorithmic accuracy.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: This model functions strictly on the 3-Minute Timeframe and above (3m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, Daily).

Absolute Restriction: Never execute this strategy on 1-minute or 2-minute charts, as micro-timeframe price noise degrades the accuracy of the absorption filters.

Step 2: Same-Timeframe FVG Mapping

Locate the core institutional liquidity matrix on your active trading chart.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Identify a valid Fair Value Gap directly on your chosen timeframe.

Key Levels to Watch: Mark the upper and lower boundaries of the FVG. This serves as your fundamental institutional anchor zone.

Step 3: Structural Liquidity Identification

Locate a key swing point relative to your identified FVG area.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Map a clear structural Swing Low or Swing High located either near or directly inside the FVG.

The Swing Fractal Rule: A valid swing low must display a strict 3-candle architecture where the center candle prints a lower low than both the preceding and succeeding candles.

Step 4: The Absorption Sweep Trigger

Wait for the market to run a highly aggressive stop-hunt sweep into your primary key levels.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Price must expand into the FVG pool and actively sweep past your selected swing point line.

Key Levels to Watch: The precise horizontal price line matching the absolute wick tip of the chosen swing high or low.

Step 5: The Validation Close (The Strategy Engine)

Verify institutional order absorption by evaluating the closing behavior of the sweeping candle. This is the core engine of the TBV strategy.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Monitor the very first candle that pierces the swing line.

The Bullish Absorption Rule: For a long trade, the very first candle that sweeps the swing low must reverse hard and close its body as a bullish candle (closing above its own opening price).

The Bearish Absorption Rule: For a short trade, the very first candle that sweeps the swing high must reverse hard and close its body as a bearish candle (closing below its own opening price).

Why This Matters: This specific behavior indicates that retail stop-loss orders were completely absorbed by large institutional limit blocks within that single timeframe window.

Step 6: Trade Execution Positioning

Enter a position the exact second the sweeping candle closes to confirm the absorption setup.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Execute an immediate market order at the opening print of the very next candle.

Key Levels to Watch: The closing price of the validated absorption bar.

Key Rules & Conditions

The Immediate Invalidation Rule: If the first candle to sweep the swing point fails to close as an absorption candle (e.g., it closes bearish on a swing low sweep), the trade setup is instantly voided. Do not enter on subsequent candles.

The FVG Structural Anchor Rule: The setup candle must touch or trade within a valid same-timeframe FVG. If the sweeping candle expands completely past the low of the FVG's initial reference candle, the setup is invalidated.

Session Agnostic Rule: This strategy can be traded across all major global sessions (Asia, London, and New York) and works effectively on all major asset classes, including Forex, Indices, Crypto, and Commodities.

Risk Management

Ensure stop-loss placement is anchored directly to the extreme high or low of the sweeping candle to protect against secondary tests.

The 1:1 Safety Lock Rule: For defensive trade management, you can trail your stop to break-even the moment price delivers a 1:1 risk-to-reward return, securing a risk-free position.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Institutional volume footprints can be accurately tracked within single candle windows by evaluating closing structures relative to recent liquidity sweeps.

Eliminating higher timeframe directional assumptions simplifies execution and allows traders to build a highly mechanical approach.

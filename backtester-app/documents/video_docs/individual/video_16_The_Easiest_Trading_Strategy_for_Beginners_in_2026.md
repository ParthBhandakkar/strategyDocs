Overview

This video introduces a completely mechanical mechanical trading strategy that eliminates the need for identifying a daily bias or conducting complex high-timeframe analysis. Operating on the 1-hour (1H) and 1-minute (1M) charts for Nasdaq (NQ) futures, it relies strictly on three primary confluences to execute precise daily setups.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: High Timeframe (1H) Setup & Key Level Marking

Timeframe: 1-hour chart (1H).

Asset focus: NQ (Nasdaq) futures.

Action: Wait for the 8:00 AM EST candle to complete its duration and close fully.

Marking: Immediately plot horizontal lines at the exact high and exact low of that specific 8:00 AM candle.

PD Array Identification: Look to the immediate left of the chart. Plot any key point of interest (PD Array) resting directly above the 8:00 AM high or below the 8:00 AM low. These include valid swing highs/lows, hourly order blocks (OB), or 1-hour fair value gaps (FVG).

Step 2: Lower Timeframe Execution (1M) & Market Structure Shift

Timeframe: 1-minute chart (1M).

Liquidity Hunt: Wait for the newly opened candle to sweep outside the boundaries of the 8:00 AM structure. The price must clear the 8:00 AM high (or low) and tap directly into the high-timeframe PD Array or swing level identified in Step 1.

Confirmation Criteria: Look for a clear Market Structure Shift (MSS) or a Change in the State of Delivery (CISD) on the 1-minute chart. To ensure a higher win rate, ensure that the 1-minute price candle closes back inside the original 8:00 AM high/low range line.

Entry Point: Set up a limit or market order upon a structural return into either a freshly formed 1-minute FVG or a clean breaker block. The stop loss is always positioned safely beyond the structural sweep high or low.

Key Rules & Conditions

Rule 1: Trade strictly one setup per day to completely prevent overtrading and revenge trading.

Rule 2: A valid HTF PD Array or swing level confluence must be swept alongside the 8:00 AM level; do not take raw sweeps without this background liquidity context.

Rule 3 (SMT Confluence): For an advanced confirmation layer, use SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence between NQ and ES (S&P 500). If NQ prints a higher high but ES fails to create a higher high, it confirms a high-probability institutional reversal.

Rule 4: Target the structural opposite side of the initial morning balance (the 8:00 AM candle's opposing low or high).

Always preserve logical risk parameters. If the raw structural breakdown leaves you with a poor risk-to-reward ratio (less than 1:2), use patience and wait for an optimized pullback into an open FVG or premium zone to build a minimum 1:2 or 1:3 ratio.

Never shift entries to breakeven prematurely. Let the market play out its probabilistic distributions naturally.

Overview

This systematic approach eliminates higher timeframe multi-hour bias confusion by focusing entirely on the daily liquidity boundaries set by the 8:00 AM hourly candle. Designed for volatile indices like Nasdaq, this setup targets pre-market liquidity expansions to capture quick, high-probability morning scalps.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Establish the Daily 8:00 AM Anchor

Open the 1-hour (1H) timeframe on NQ or MNQ futures.

Identify the candle that opens at 08:00 AM New York time and wait for it to close at exactly 09:00 AM.

Plot clean horizontal boundary lines matching the absolute high wick and absolute low wick of this candle.

Step 2: Map Immediate Structural Swing Points

Look closely at the adjacent historical data on your 1-hour chart.

Mark the nearest significant swing high above your 8:00 AM high line, and the nearest significant swing low resting directly beneath your 8:00 AM low line.

Step 3: Monitor Pre-Market Sweeps on the 1-Minute Chart

Downshift to the 1-minute (1M) timeframe as the market approaches the 9:30 AM New York open.

Wait for price to aggressively sweep through either your 8:00 AM high/low line AND its nearby historical structural swing high/low point.

Step 4: Validate and Execute via the Reversal Trigger

Track the 1-minute candles post-sweep. Look for an explicit Market Structure Shift (MSS) or an Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG).

Conservative Entry Trigger: Wait for a 1-minute candle to cross back and close inside the original 8:00 AM candle boundary line to confirm a liquidity sweep. Execute your position on the retest of the breakout point or the local 1-minute Fair Value Gap. Target the opposing 8:00 AM high or low line.

Key Rules & Conditions

Set-and-Forget Execution: Once your entry is triggered and your stop loss is placed past the extreme low or high of the sweep, close your trading platform. Do not micro-manage or manually move your stop loss to break-even prematurely.

Minimum Reward Target: Never accept a trade setup that yields less than a 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio to the opposing candle boundary line.

Correlation Filter: Check for a 1-minute SMT divergence between NQ and ES during the sweep phase to confirm a high-probability reversal.

Risk Management

The Set-and-Forget Rule: Do not modify your stop loss or move it to break-even mid-trade based on short-term market noise. Let the trade hit your structural stop or your take-profit target to ensure long-term statistical consistency.

The 8:00 AM hourly candle acts as a reliable volatility anchor for index traders.

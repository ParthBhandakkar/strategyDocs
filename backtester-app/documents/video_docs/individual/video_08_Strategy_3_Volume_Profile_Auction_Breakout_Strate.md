Overview

This detailed guide moves away from pure candle charting to focus on price distribution using volume profiles. It outlines two distinct setups: the Failed Auction Setup (mean-reversion back inside value fields) and the Breakout Setup (momentum trades following the acceptance of new value).

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Technical Indicator Configuration

Deploy the Fixed Range Volume Profile (FRVP) tracking tool on your chart layout.

Internal Setting Adjustments:

Row Size: 1,000

Value Area Volume: 70%

Visuals: Toggle visibility to ON for Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and Point of Control (POC).

Extend the volume profile display rightward across developing sessions to capture expanding volume distributions in real time.

Step 2: Establish the Structural Bias via Volume Profile Topology

Examine the shape of the evolving daily volume profile distribution to determine market conditions:

D-Shape profile: Highly balanced distribution layout. Represents a neutral bias; focus strategy on trading Failed Auction mean-reversions back and forth between value ranges.

P-Shape profile: High volume concentration shifted toward the upper boundary. Represents a bullish bias; prioritize long breakouts above VAH lines.

b-Shape profile: High volume concentration shifted toward the lower boundary. Represents a bearish bias; prioritize short breakouts below VAL lines.

Step 3A: Execution Rules for the Failed Auction Setup

Wait for a 5-minute candle to print a close entirely outside the primary Value Area thresholds (crossing above the VAH line or below the VAL line).

Look for an immediate counter-move where a subsequent 5-minute candle closes back inside the Value Area boundaries.

Drop to the 1-minute chart to confirm heavy volume accumulation (e.g., massive cluster alerts on the Absorption Bubbles tool showing aggressive orders getting halted by hidden institutional limits).

Open a trade targeting the POC midline as your primary target area.

Step 3B: Execution Rules for the Breakout Setup

Observe price trading beyond either the VAH or VAL boundary lines.

Instead of returning inside, price stays outside and begins to consolidate heavily, forming a new value base.

This confirms that market participants are accepting these newer, higher or lower prices as fair value.

Enter a breakout position once the asset breaks out past the outer boundary of this new consolidation zone, using clear order flow tools to verify the move.

Key Rules & Conditions

Session Anchor: For Asia session trading models, track and build your developing volume profile starting exactly at 6:00 PM New York Time. Allow volume to build for a minimum of 30 minutes before taking trade signals.

Frequency Limits: Limit execution to a maximum of two trade setups per day for Failed Auction strategies. Continuous retesting usually indicates that a major trend breakout is building.

POC Confluence: Avoid entering counter-trend trades if the daily POC sits too close to the outer VAH or VAL lines, as this limits the necessary room for structural targets.

Risk Management

Always trail your open risk profiles to a strict break-even protective setting once price reaches the POC line.

If you use the Absorption Bubbles indicator, place your stop loss orders directly behind the largest volume bubbles to use institutional positions as structural protection.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Volume Profiles show exactly where major market participants view prices as fair or unfair.

Understanding the profile's shape provides a clear directional bias for the session.

Successful trading relies on identifying whether prices outside of value face rejection or find acceptance.

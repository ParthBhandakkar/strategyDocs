Overview

This strategy focuses on 15-minute and above timeframes for trading crypto using range identification, liquidity sweeps, and strict risk management.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Range

Identify a push and a pullback to define the Range High and Range Low.

A valid range requires a market structure shift that creates the high or low.

Step 2: Trade Breakout/Reversal

Wait for price to sweep the high or low.

Look for a market structure shift and a close back within the range.

Enter from an auto block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Use 15-minute timeframe or higher.

Apply strict risk management (partials).

Summary / Key Takeaways

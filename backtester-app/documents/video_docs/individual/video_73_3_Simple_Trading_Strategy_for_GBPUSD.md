3. Simple Trading Strategy for GBPUSD

Source: Faiz SMC ("This boring trading strategy made me $6,620 in a few minutes..")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LPmmymPRpno

Overview

A detailed breakdown of a trade that earned $6,620, using a strategy based on higher timeframe order flow, SMT (Smart Money Technique) divergence, and liquidity sweeps on the 15-minute timeframe.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Higher Timeframe Analysis

Go to the 4-hour timeframe to identify the overall market direction (order flow).

If direction is unclear within 3–4 seconds, move to a different pair.

Step 2: Mark Key Levels (PD Arrays)

Identify Order Blocks and FVG on the 4-hour timeframe. Wait for price to tap into these levels.

Step 3: Confirm with SMT Divergence

On the 15-minute timeframe, when price reaches a PD array, look for SMT Divergence (e.g., if one pair makes a lower low but the other does not).

Step 4: Execution

Wait for a liquidity sweep of a short-term low/high.

Enter when the price closes above/below the body of the candle that swept the liquidity (acting as your entry order block).

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade the stronger pair (the one that did not make the lower low/high in SMT).

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (GBPUSD Trade):

Market direction: Up. Price tapped into a 4-hour order block with equal highs nearby.

Divergence: EURUSD made a lower low, but GBPUSD did not.

Entry: On the retest of the bearish candle that swept the low.

Outcome: 1:2.5 risk-to-reward ratio.

Risk Management

Standard reward-to-risk ratio goal: 1:2.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The core of this strategy is combining trend-following with SMT divergence as a confirmation tool.

SMT Divergence acts as an extra confirmation to avoid "fake-outs."

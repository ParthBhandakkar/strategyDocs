Overview

This video outlines a strategy based on the "Power of Three" concept (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) to determine directional bias using weekly and daily timeframes. It explains how to align lower timeframes to catch expansion moves.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Determining Bias

Use the first two candles of a three-candle pattern on a weekly or daily chart to establish directional bias.

Bullish Bias: If the second candle closes above the first candle's high.

Bearish Bias: If the second candle closes below the first candle's low.

Step 2: Trading the Third Candle

Mark the opening price of the third candle.

Weekly Strategy: Drop to a 4-hour timeframe, identify liquidity (old highs/lows) on the left of the opening price, wait for a liquidity sweep (manipulation), and then drop to a 15-minute timeframe for a market structure shift and entry.

Daily Strategy: Similar process but using a 1-hour timeframe for liquidity and a 5-minute timeframe for entries.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only trade in the direction of the established bias.

For weekly bias, use 4-hour timeframe for liquidity and 15-minute for entries.

For daily bias, use 1-hour timeframe for liquidity and 5-minute for entries.

Avoid trading on Fridays as distribution moves are limited.

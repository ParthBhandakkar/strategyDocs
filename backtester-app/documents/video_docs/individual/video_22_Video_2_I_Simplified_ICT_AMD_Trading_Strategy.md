Wait for price to push violently past the open level. Use a Fibonacci tool anchored from the last valid structural high to the last structural low prior to the opening range's market structure shift to track the manipulation move.

The standard target area where manipulation exhaustively ends resides precisely between the -2.0 and -2.5 standard deviation lines.

Anchor your Fibonacci tool across the new execution swing leg (from the true manipulation low/high to the shift pivot high/low). Look to lock in your profits dynamically inside the -2.0 and -2.5 extensions, ensuring they line up with significant pool lows or highs from earlier charts.

Timeframe Flexibility: Use the 1-minute timeframe during standard high-volume sessions. If the historical data block looks choppy, shift to the 5-minute timeframe to filter out market noise.

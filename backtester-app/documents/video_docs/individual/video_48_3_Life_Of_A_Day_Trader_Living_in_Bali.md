Source: Faiz SMC ("Life Of A Day Trader Living in Bali")

Overview

This video is a blend of vlog content and trading strategy. It discusses the lifestyle of a trader in Bali and breaks down the exact trade execution model used to achieve a winning streak in the Discord community.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Bias/Context

Start on the daily timeframe to see market structure (liquidity sweeps).

Price moves from external (Highs/Lows) to internal (FVGs) liquidity.

Step 2: The Model

Use 15-minute timeframe for significant levels/FVGs before the market open (09:30 AM).

Drop to 1-minute timeframe for entries.

Within 1-minute dealing range, check 1, 2, 3-minute timeframes for FVGs.

Take the entry on the highest timeframe inversion that contains an FVG (within the 5-minute limit).

Key Rules & Conditions

Use 15-minute key levels as a guide.

Stick to the highest timeframe inversion within the dealing range.

Break even immediately after the closest liquidity is hit.

Use Hammer/Magnet techniques if needed for manual entry/exit.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Entry: Short position.

Stop Loss: Break even.

Outcome: W (Full TP hit).

Risk Management

Move to break even as soon as the closest liquidity level is hit.

Use 1:1 for partials (if trading multiple contracts).

Target 1:2 RRR.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading success comes from personal experience (forward testing/backtesting).

Stick to one model and master it.

Focus on capital protection over profit maximization.

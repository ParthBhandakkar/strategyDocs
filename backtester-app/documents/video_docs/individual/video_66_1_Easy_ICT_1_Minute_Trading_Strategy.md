Overview

This video outlines a mechanical 1-minute trading strategy focusing on NQ, emphasizing the importance of liquidity sweeps, SMT divergence, and fair value gaps for high-win-rate trades.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Market Analysis

Start on the daily timeframe to gauge the market's rough bias for the day, specifically checking for liquidity sweeps or autoblocks.

Step 2: 15-Minute Fair Value Gap Identification

During the 9:30 am - 11:30 am trading window, identify and mark out fair value gaps (FVG) on the 15-minute timeframe.

Step 3: SMT Divergence (1-Minute)

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe within the 15-minute FVG and look for SMT divergence between NQ and a correlating asset (e.g., ES).

Step 4: Inverse Fair Value Gap Entry

Wait for price to inverse the 1-minute FVG within the dealing range and close above (for long) or below (for short) the FVG. The close is the entry trigger.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trading window: 9:30 am – 11:30 am EST.

Avoid trading during red-folder news events.

SMT Divergence: NQ should fail to create a lower low when ES does.

A+ Setup: Price taps into a 15-minute FVG, then shows 1-minute SMT and FVG inversion.

A Setup: Just 1-minute SMT and FVG inversion (without tapping into 15-minute FVG).

Trade Examples From The Video

Asset/Pair: NQ

Entry: Inversion of the last FVG.

Risk Management

Go break even when the closest liquidity pool is swept.

Take 1st partial at 1:1 RR.

Take 2nd partial at 1:2 RR.

Final TP is towards pre-marked equal highs/lows.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Always follow the mechanical risk management rules.

SMT divergence is crucial for increasing the probability of a trade setup.

4. ICT Market Maker Model

Source: Faiz SMC ("Make 'F*ck You' Money With This Simple ICT MMXM Strategy")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=06DxvYmOXP4

Overview

This video introduces the Market Maker Sell/Buy Model (MMXM) and teaches how to trade the reversal after liquidity has been swept, focusing on high-probability intraday setups.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: High Timeframe Order Flow (4 Hour)

Identify the main trend (bullish or bearish) on the 4-hour timeframe.

Step 2: Liquidity Sweep

Mark the lowest (in bullish trend) or highest (in bearish trend) point before a market structure break. Wait for price to sweep this liquidity level.

Step 3: 15-Minute Market Structure Shift

After the sweep, look for a 15-minute market structure shift with strong displacement.

Step 4: Entry Model

Take the entry on a retest of a fair value gap or breaker block formed during the shift.

Key Rules & Conditions

High timeframe trend is critical for higher probability.

If price doesn't close above the high/low on the 4-hour chart, do not consider it a valid break of structure.

Trading during London or New York Killzone hours is mandatory.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: NZDUSD

Setup: 1-hour downtrend, sweep of liquidity, 5-minute market structure shift.

Outcome: 7 RR trade.

Risk Management

Stop loss above/below the autoblock.

Target the original consolidation.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The liquidity levels act as magnets for price.

Trading with the high-timeframe trend significantly increases probability.

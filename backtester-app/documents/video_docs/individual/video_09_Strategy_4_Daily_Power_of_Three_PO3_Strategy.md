Overview

This strategy simplifies the classic Inner Circle Trader (ICT) Power of Three model, which focuses on the Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution cycle. By focusing on the 10:00 AM New York 4-hour candle open, this approach captures the precise moments when major candle wicks are engineered.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Plot the 10 AM Open Price Anchor

Ensure your platform's time zone tracking matches New York Time.

Identify the exact open print of the 10:00 AM New York 4-hour candle.

Draw a prominent horizontal line across this price level to serve as your session anchor.

Step 2: Map Structural Reference Levels on the 15-Minute Chart

Drop down to the 15-minute chart to identify nearby liquidity levels on the left side of your chart history:

Locate the most recent, clean 15-minute swing highs and swing lows.

Mark out any fresh, unfilled 15-minute fair value gaps. These technical zones mark where price manipulation is likely to reverse.

Step 3: Identify the Manipulation Phase via SMT Divergence

After the 10:00 AM open, look for price to expand away from the anchor line in a classic manipulation leg. Price must break past a 15-minute structural level or tap into an FVG.

Compare NQ (Nasdaq) and ES (S&P 500) closely at this reversal pivot to find an SMT Divergence.

For example, if you are looking for shorts, you want to see NQ sweep past a structural high to create a fresh higher-high, while ES fails to break its corresponding high pivot. This structural divergence confirms an active manipulation phase.

Step 4: Confirm Entry on the 1-Minute Chart

Once the SMT divergence prints inside your 15-minute technical zone, drop down to the 1-minute chart for entry signals.

Wait for a clear Change in the State of Delivery (CISD) or a distinct 1-minute Breaker Block formation.

Enter a market position immediately on the structural shift close or on the subsequent retest of the breaker block boundary.

Key Rules & Conditions

Directional Bias Filter: Establish your session bias by analyzing the preceding 6:00 AM New York 4-hour candle. If the 6:00 AM candle swept buy-side liquidity and left a clear upside wick rejection, prepare for a bearish PO3 expansion on the 10:00 AM candle.

Execution Rules: Always place your protective stops directly above or below the established SMT high or low point. This ensures your risk parameters are protected by institutional execution levels.

Risk Management

Use the Fibonacci tool to manage targets mechanically. Draw it from the manipulation high/low point down to the structural pivot preceding the market structure shift. Lock in your main profit targets at the -2.0 and -2.5 standard deviation expansion marks.

Manage open risk by taking regular partial profits or moving stops to break-even once price reaches a 1:1.5 risk-to-reward ratio.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Since nearly all major 4-hour candles develop upper or lower wicks, you can use the PO3 model to capitalize on those predictable wick expansions.

Requiring an SMT divergence prevents you from entering trades early during strong breakout trends.

Using Fibonacci standard deviations provides objective, rule-based profit targets that remove emotion from trade management.

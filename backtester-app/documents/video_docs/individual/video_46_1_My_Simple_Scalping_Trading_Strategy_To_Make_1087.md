Overview

This video outlines a simple scalping strategy based on high-timeframe (HTF) Fair Value Gaps (FVG) and order flow. It emphasizes avoiding complex models like quarterly theory or standard deviations, focusing instead on consistent application of a specific entry model during the 09:30 AM – 11:30 AM New York session.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify HTF Bias

Check the daily timeframe to determine the overall trend (are we inside an FVG, or have we taken out a major high/low?).

The 15-minute timeframe is used for immediate directional bias. If the trend is bullish, look for long setups; if bearish, look for short setups.

Step 2: Mark HTF Key Levels

After 09:30 AM, identify the 15-minute FVG (bullish if trending up, bearish if trending down).

Note external liquidity levels (e.g., Asia high/low, London high/low, or previous day high/low) which act as potential "draws on liquidity" (targets).

Step 3: Lower Timeframe (LTF) Confirmation

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe once price enters the 15-minute FVG.

Mark the 1-minute dealing range (high to low).

Check for SMT Divergence: Check ES vs NQ. If NQ makes a new lower low and ES fails to do so (or vice versa), this provides extra confidence.

Identify Inversion: Look for the highest timeframe FVG (check 1min, 2min, 3min, 4min, 5min—5min is the cap) within that dealing range. Once price breaks and closes above/below that specific FVG (inversion), it is the entry signal.

Step 4: Execution & Risk Management

Entry: On candle closure above/below the highest timeframe FVG.

Stop Loss: Below the body of the candle (not the wick) to keep the SL small.

Break Even: As soon as the closest liquidity level (buy-side or sell-side) is taken, move stop to break even.

Partial/TP: Take 30% partial at 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio. Close remaining at 1:2.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only trade 09:30 AM – 11:30 AM New York time.

Use the highest timeframe FVG (up to 5 min) for the inversion signal.

Be aggressive with moving to break even once liquidity is hit.

Do not chase trades if they move beyond 1:2 RRR.

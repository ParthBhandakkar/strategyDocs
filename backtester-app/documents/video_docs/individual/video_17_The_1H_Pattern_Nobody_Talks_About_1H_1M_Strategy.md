Overview

This framework explains the internal mechanics of how individual candles develop using the classic "Power of Three" concept (Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution). By identifying historical high/low anchor points on the 5-minute chart right before a new 1-hour candle opens, traders can utilize precise Fibonacci standard deviation projections to accurately target the exact point where internal candle manipulation shifts into trend distribution.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Open Price Anchoring & High Timeframe Isolation

Timeframe: 1-hour chart (1H).

Action: As soon as a fresh 1-hour candle prints its initial opening tick, draw a solid horizontal line across the screen marking that exact opening valuation.

Session-candle targets: Focus on specific session anchors: 8:00 PM candle for Asia, 4:00 AM candle for London, and 8:00 AM candle for the New York session.

Step 2: 5-Minute Structural Boundary Mapping

Timeframe: Switch down to the 5-minute chart (5M).

Action: Scan directly to the left of your marked hourly open price line. Identify and highlight the absolute closest, unswept 5-minute swing high and the closest unswept 5-minute swing low. These levels form the immediate structural playing field for the strategy.

Step 3: 1-Minute Manipulation Analysis & Standard Deviation Projections

Timeframe: Switch down to the 1-minute chart (1M).

Action: Observe price as it sweeps past the 5-minute structural boundaries (high or low).

Fibonacci Confluence Tool: To calculate the structural end of this manipulation phase, draw a Fibonacci retracement layer using a precise structural swing point that broke down right before the open. Measure from the body of its swing high to the body of its swing low.

Fibonacci Target Settings: Ensure your Fibonacci tool uses these precise project coordinates: -2.0, -2.5, and -4.0.

Step 4: Reversal Confirmation & Entry

Action: Wait for price to tap into the standard deviation target zone located precisely between the -2.0 and -2.5 extensions. This must line up closely with the swept 5-minute liquidity level.

Trigger: Look for a 1-minute market structure shift (MSS) or a strong clear inversion of a 1-minute fair value gap. Enter on the immediate structural retest of the FVG or order block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Rule 1: The target 5-minute high or low level must be completely fresh and unswept prior to the current 1-hour candle's open.

Rule 2: Avoid hunting for trend entries if the price punches straight past the calculated -2.0 standard deviation marker without clean reversal behavior.

Rule 3: Align the Fibonacci tool with the structural candles' bodies to ensure clear algorithmic precision.

Rule 4: Limit execution paths to one highly optimized trade per major global market session.

Risk Management

The strategy manages risk profiles by establishing fixed 1:2 Risk-to-Reward parameters across standard trades.

If standard deviation coordinates point to targets that are too far away for the current hourly session volume, adjust target metrics to safer internal liquidity targets or close out positions before the 1-hour candle finishes.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The market moves through repeating cycles of accumulation, manipulation, and distribution across all timeframes.

Standard deviation projections offer systematic validation of exactly where a market stop-run is likely to end.

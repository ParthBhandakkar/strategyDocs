Overview

This video outlines an Asian and London session Judas swing strategy designed for those with busy schedules (like a 9-5 job). It is a mechanical strategy that relies on trading at specific times.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Asian/London Ranges

Indicator: Asian Session Range (by Rob Minty).

Asian Session Timing: 8:00 PM – 2:00 AM (New York time).

London Session Timing: 3:00 AM – 7:00 AM (New York time).

Process: Mark the highest and lowest points of the Asian/London range.

Step 2: Wait for Liquidity Sweep

Asian Liquidity Sweep Window: 2:00 AM – 3:00 AM.

London Liquidity Sweep Window: 7:00 AM – 8:00 AM.

Process: Wait for price to sweep either the Asian/London session high or low during the designated window. If it doesn't happen, the setup is invalid.

Step 3: Entry and Management

Process: After the sweep, look for a market structure shift with a body closure on the 5-minute timeframe.

Entry: Enter from an order block or fair value gap.

Stop Loss: Place above/below the high/low created by the sweep.

Risk Management: Go break even at 1:1 reward-to-risk ratio, take a partial (e.g., 30%), and target the opposite range high/low.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only trade EURUSD and GBPUSD.

Only trade during killzone hours for the best win rate.

Setup is invalid if price closes back inside the range without a market structure shift.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (EURUSD):

Setup: Asian high/low marked. Liquidity sweep of Asian low between 2:00 AM and 3:00 AM.

Entry: Market structure shift on 5-minute chart, entry at order block/fair value gap.

Outcome: Price pushed up to Asian range high.

Example 2 (EURUSD):

Setup: Similar to Example 1, but with London range.

Sweep: Liquidity sweep of London low between 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM.

Outcome: Multiple RR achieved.

Risk Management

Go break even at 1:1.

Take profits at 1:2 or hold until the opposite session high/low is reached.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The strategy works best on EURUSD and GBPUSD.

Use a 1-hour timeframe to identify order flow (trend).

If the trend is unclear within 3 seconds of opening the chart, skip that pair.

Buying in a discount (below 50% of the dealing range) increases the probability of success.

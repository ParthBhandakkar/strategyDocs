4. Backtesting Tutorial

Source: Faiz SMC ("Increase Your Trading Skills By 276% (Backtesting Tutorial)")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fr2dDjvWNW0

Overview

This video demonstrates how to use FX Replay for effective backtesting, emphasizing that a robust trading edge is built through data collection rather than just guessing.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Setting Up the Session

Create a session in FX Replay with your account balance and pair.

Add a new strategy (e.g., "Opening Range Breakout").

Step 2: Backtesting Process

Use the replay tool to simulate price action candle-by-candle (1-minute or 5-minute).

Mark out specific ranges (e.g., the first 9:30 AM candle).

Identify breakouts and place buy/sell limit orders automatically.

Step 3: Data Collection

Use the "graph" icon on the FX Replay dashboard to see win rates, average risk-to-reward, and performance per day/session.

Key Rules & Conditions

Do not use community indicators; stick to essential data or session indicators.

Use the tool’s economic calendar to account for news impact.

Use custom session times to focus only on relevant trading hours (e.g., 9:30 AM open).

Trade Examples From The Video

The author demonstrates several test trades using an "Opening Range Breakout" strategy on NQ (Nasdaq) to show the mechanics of the tool.

Risk Management

The tool defaults to risking 1% per trade.

The platform automatically calculates and sets stop-loss and take-profit levels.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Backtesting is the secret tool of the top 3-5% of traders.

A strategy without backtested data is worthless.

Using specialized software like FX Replay allows for rapid data collection compared to manual backtesting.

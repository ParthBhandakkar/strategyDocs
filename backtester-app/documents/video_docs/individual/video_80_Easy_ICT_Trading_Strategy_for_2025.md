Overview

This video provides a deep dive into the ICT Power of 3 (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) strategy, focusing on its application on higher timeframes (Weekly and Daily) to achieve consistent profitability.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Bias

Timeframe: Daily or Weekly.

Process: Use the previous two candles to determine the bias for the third.

Rules:

If the second candle closes above the first, the third candle bias is bullish.

If the second candle fails to close above/below the first, the third candle bias is bearish (or vice versa).

Step 2: Identify Manipulation and Expansion

Timeframe: 15-minute (for daily bias) or 1-hour (for weekly bias).

Process: Mark the opening price of the third day/week.

Liquidity: Look for liquidity to the left of the opening price (short-term highs/lows, equal highs/lows).

Manipulation: Wait for price to sweep this liquidity. Use Fibonacci (last high to last low) to confirm the manipulation, which typically ends at -2 or -2.5 standard deviations.

Step 3: Entry and Management

Process: Wait for a market structure shift after the manipulation, then enter at the order block.

Risk Management: Target 1:2 reward-to-risk ratio.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade only with the bias (Weekly/Daily).

If the second candle fails to close above the first (for bullish) or below the first (for bearish), the bias is flipped.

The strategy is most effective on Futures.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The strategy works on all pairs/assets, but Futures are recommended for cleaner price action.

Higher timeframe order flow (Daily/Weekly) is more significant than lower timeframe data.

The strategy is not magic; losing is part of trading. Consistency and mindset (meditation/mindfulness) are key.

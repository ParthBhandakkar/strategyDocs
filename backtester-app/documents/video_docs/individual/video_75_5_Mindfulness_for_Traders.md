5. Mindfulness for Traders

Source: Faiz SMC ("Give me 6 minutes of your life, and I’ll make you a better trader forever")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zqRQ3b0a0Sc

Overview

A focus on psychological development, arguing that trading success is heavily dependent on a disciplined, focused mindset, which can be improved through mindfulness and meditation.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Reducing External Stimuli

Decrease daily screen time and social media usage (which distracts the mind).

Step 2: Practicing Mindfulness/Meditation

Manually control your breathing: breathe in for 5 seconds, breathe out for 5 seconds.

Focus entirely on the physical sensation of breathing to anchor the mind in the present moment.

Start with 5 minutes a day.

Step 3: Correcting Wandering Thoughts

When the mind drifts to past stressors, future worries, or unrelated thoughts, gently bring focus back to the breath.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trading is one of the hardest businesses; it requires high discipline.

If your mind is scattered, you will overtrade to seek a "dopamine hit" (seeing blue numbers) rather than taking high-probability setups.

Be grateful for what you have to lower stress levels.

Risk Management

N/A (Focus is on internal discipline).

Summary / Key Takeaways

The best strategy in the world will fail in the hands of a trader with an undisciplined mind.

Mindfulness training (meditation and breathing) helps a trader remain in the present moment.

Success comes from mastering yourself, not just the market.

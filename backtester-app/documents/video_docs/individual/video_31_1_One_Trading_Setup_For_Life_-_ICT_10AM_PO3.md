Overview

This video introduces a rule-based, mechanical trading model built around the ICT Power of Three (PO3) mechanism utilizing the 4-hour time frame and execution on lower time frames. Specifically, it maps out a strict method for defining daily directional bias by using the structural relationship between the 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM candles, identifying the exact zone where the 10:00 AM candle's manipulation ends, and selecting high probability targets.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Establish Your Daily Bias (4-Hour Time Frame)

Before taking any execution steps, a mechanical bias must be established on the 4-hour chart using the structural relationship between the 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM candles:

Bullish Bias: If the 6:00 AM candle closes explicitly above the high of the 2:00 AM candle.

Bearish Bias: If the 6:00 AM candle closes explicitly below the low of the 2:00 AM candle.

Rejection Scenario: If the 6:00 AM candle wicks above the 2:00 AM high but fails to close above it (closing back within or below), the bias becomes bearish. Conversely, if it wicks below the 2:00 AM low but closes back above it, the bias flips bullish.

No-Trade Rule: If the 6:00 AM candle wicks beyond both the high and the low of the 2:00 AM candle, you must stand still, do not trade, and wait for the next day.

Step 2: Mark the 10:00 AM Candle Opening Price

Once the 10:00 AM 4-hour candle opens, mark its precise opening price level on your chart. This acts as the baseline for the Power of Three dynamic (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution):

For a Bullish Bias, the manipulation leg must plunge below the opening price to seek liquidity before distributing upward.

For a Bearish Bias, the manipulation leg must rally above the opening price to seek liquidity before distributing downward.

Step 3: Identify Lower Time Frame Liquidity arrays (15-Minute Chart)

Immediately drop down to the 15-minute time frame to plot the specific targets where the manipulation phase is likely to terminate:

Look for unmitigated, pristine 15-minute Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) resting below the opening price for longs, or above the opening price for shorts.

If no 15-minute FVGs are available, refine down to a clean 5-minute FVG.

Alternatively, note key liquidity arrays such as the London session session high/low, Asian session high/low, or any highly significant 15-minute structural key low or high.

Step 4: Map Out Manipulation Endpoints with Standard Deviation

To identify the endpoint of the manipulation leg with maximum precision, use the Fibonacci extension tool to project standard deviations:

Measure from the last key swing high to the last key swing low printed within the immediate accumulation phase prior to the displacement or structure shift.

The expansion target for the final manipulation swipe routinely tops out precisely between the -2.0 and -2.5 standard deviation levels. When these extensions align seamlessly inside your lower-timeframe FVG or liquidity key array, it creates a prime setup.

Step 5: Lower Time Frame Confirmation and Entry (1-Minute Chart)

Once price enters the designated lower time frame FVG, or sweeps the key liquidity array at the projected standard deviation levels, drop down to the 1-minute chart:

Execution Model A: Look for a classic 2022 ICT entry consisting of a clean market structure shift (MSS) confirmed by a body closure, followed by a limit entry at the newly formed 1-minute FVG, breaker block, or order block.

Execution Model B: Look for an inversion fair value gap setup where multiple continuous lower-timeframe gaps are completely overridden and closed past by reversing price action.

Key Rules & Conditions

Asset/Contract Restriction: This setup is designed specifically for futures markets only (e.g., NQ Nasdaq futures, Gold futures).

Time Zone Tracking: All tracking hours rely entirely on New York local time. If trading CFDs or Forex, the timing alignments on MT4/MT5 platforms will display a 9:00 AM window instead of the correct 10:00 AM futures open due to platform metric variances; therefore, standard spot Forex/CFDs are not recommended.

Favorable FVG Filter: Only trade institutional arrays that stay completely valid relative to the opening price. For long setups, the bullish FVG must reside completely below the opening price. For short setups, the bearish FVG must reside completely above the opening price.

Strategy/Topic Name: Day Trading for Profit

Source: Faiz SMC ("How I Made $2,600 In 10 Minutes Day Trading")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FtDqcK4TUME

Overview

This video breaks down a live trade executed by the creator. It emphasizes building a "story" (context) on higher timeframes (daily/1-hour) before executing on a lower timeframe (2-minute) based on liquidity levels.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Higher Timeframe Analysis (Daily/1-Hour)

Check the daily timeframe to identify external vs. internal range liquidity.

Check for volume imbalances that price is currently respecting.

Establish a "story" or direction: Moving from external to internal liquidity (or vice versa).

Step 2: 1-Hour Analysis & Short-Term Bias

On the 1-hour, look for order flow.

Look for equal highs or lows engineered as "draw on liquidity."

Step 3: Execution (2-Minute)

Analyze the 9:30 AM market open.

Identify "fake" market structure shifts occurring before 9:30 AM.

Wait for a "true" sweep after the market opens.

Look for inversion of a fair value gap (FVG).

Key Rules & Conditions

Do not trade before the 9:30 AM New York open; displacements before this time are often fake.

If price closes above an FVG, it becomes an "inversion FVG" (a signal for entry).

Highs and lows are valid PD arrays.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: Nasdaq 100 Futures

Date: November 26

Entry: Long position taken upon the inversion of a 2-minute FVG after a liquidity sweep post-9:30 AM.

Outcome: $2,600 profit.

Risk Management

Creator targets 1:2 to 1:3 risk-to-reward ratios.

Does not hold trades for extended periods.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Market makers engineer liquidity; look for "draw on liquidity" targets.

Build a narrative on higher timeframes before zooming into execution.

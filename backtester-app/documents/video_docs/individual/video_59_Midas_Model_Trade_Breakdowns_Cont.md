Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)

1-Minute Variation: (Follows the same logic as previous breakdowns: 15-min high/low setup -> sweep -> 1-min market structure shift with displacement -> entry).

5-Minute Variation:

After 8:00 PM, identify the most recent high and low on the 5-minute timeframe.

Wait for an obvious run on liquidity (a leg that sweeps a high/low and leaves Fair Value Gaps).

Once the sweep occurs, wait for price to inverse the Fair Value Gap within the dealing range.

Enter once the Fair Value Gap is closed above/below.

9:30 AM Model: Apply the 5-minute variation logic to the market session opening at 9:30 AM New York time.

Key Rules & Conditions

Move to break-even as soon as the closest liquidity pool is taken.

If multiple Fair Value Gaps exist within a leg, wait for all of them to be inversed.

Strictly adhere to the break-even rule.

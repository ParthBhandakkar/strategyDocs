Strategy/Topic Name: The 1-Candle Hourly Range Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("one candle is all you need..")

Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osc_hFXmQZA

Overview

This strategy provides a rule-based, mechanical approach to trading by focusing entirely on a single hourly candle range to define daily support and resistance boundaries. It eliminates indicators, complex order blocks, and multi-timeframe daily bias tracking, relying instead on algorithmic expansions that occur around the New York market open.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Set the Time Zone and Isolate the Reference Candle

Timeframes Used: 1-Hour (1H) chart.

Key Conditions, Settings, Timeframes: Ensure the TradingView chart time zone is set to New York time.

For Gold (XAUUSD), isolate the 7:00 AM New York time 1-hour candle.

For NQ Futures, isolate the 8:00 AM New York time 1-hour candle.

Key Levels to Watch: Use horizontal rays to mark the exact structural high and low of this specific hourly candle. This high and low define your operational trading range for the session.

Step 2: Wait for a Liquidity Sweep of the Range Boundaries

Timeframes Used: 1-Hour (1H) chart to monitor the sweep.

Key Levels to Watch: The previously marked 7:00 AM (or 8:00 AM) range high and low. Monitor the subsequent candles to see if they break past these boundaries to purge liquidity.

Step 3: Transition to the Execution Chart and Identify a Reversal Signature

Timeframes Used: 2-Minute (2M) chart. Do not use the 1-minute chart, as Gold's volatility can generate false structural readings.

Execution Triggers: Once a range boundary is swept, watch the 2M chart for two sequential conditions:

Change in a State of Delivery (CISD): Locate the consecutive group of bullish or bearish candles that drove the initial sweep. Wait for a 2M candle to close past the body of the first candle in that consecutive sequence.

Range Re-entry: The validating 2M candle must close back inside the established hourly range boundary.

Entry Method: Enter a market order immediately upon the 2M validation close, or place a limit order at the 50% retracement level of the expansion leg using a Fibonacci tool.

Key Rules & Conditions

Setup Classifications:

A-Setup (Standard): Executed purely based on a range boundary sweep followed by a lower-timeframe structural shift.

A+ Setup (Premium): Occurs when the hourly range high or low perfectly aligns with an unmitigated 1-hour institutional order block or fair value gap. This alignment adds higher-timeframe confluence to the trade.

Target Mechanics: The ultimate target is the opposing side of the hourly range (e.g., if the low is swept, target the range high).

Consistency vs. Perfection: This strategy accepts a realistic win rate of 50–60%, relying on a strong risk-to-reward ratio to maintain profitability.

Risk Management

Partial Profit Plan: Secure a partial profit at a 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio. Once locked in, move the stop loss to break-even to protect your capital.

Mid-Wick Stop Adjustments: If the manipulation wick is exceptionally large due to news volatility, avoid placing the stop below the absolute low. Instead, place it at the 50% midpoint of the wick to maintain a favorable risk-to-reward profile.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Successful trading does not require a high win rate; a 50% win rate paired with a strong risk-to-reward ratio provides a consistent trading edge.

Isolating a single hourly candle range helps filter out market noise and uncovers clear institutional liquidity pools.

Avoid the 1-minute chart when trading volatile assets like Gold; use the 2-minute chart to find reliable structural shifts.

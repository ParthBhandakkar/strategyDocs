Overview

A trade breakdown of a strategy that resulted in a significant profit. It covers the strategy used for both Forex and Futures (NQ/ES). It focuses on identifying higher time frame (HTF) points of interest (POI) and using lower time frame (LTF) SMT divergence and FVG inversions for entries.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify HTF Point of Interest

Forex: Use 1h/4h time frame to identify a FVG as a POI.

Futures: Use 15m time frame to identify HTF POI (can use highs/lows or FVGs).

Step 2: LTF Entry

Forex (5m TF): Wait for price to tap into the HTF FVG. Look for SMT divergence with a correlating pair (e.g., EU/GU). Identify FVG within the LTF dealing range, wait for inversion, and enter.

Futures (1m TF): Look for SMT divergence after an initial liquidity sweep of short-term highs/lows. Enter based on FVG or breaker block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Forex HTF: 1h/4h FVG.

Futures HTF: 15m high/low.

Partial/BE: 1:1 RR partial (30-40%) and move to BE.

Invalidation: If closest liquidity level is hit before entry, setup is invalid.

Risk Management

Aim for 1:2 to 1:2.5 RR.

30-40% partial at 1:1 RR, move stops to break even.

For challenges, can risk 2-3% per trade (not recommended for funded accounts).

Summary / Key Takeaways

HTF POI is crucial for defining the trade direction.

LTF SMT divergence validates the trade setup.

FVG inversion provides the precise entry point.

Risk management is paramount, adjusting based on account type (challenge vs. funded).

Overview

This tutorial provides an in-depth breakdown of the proprietary 3-Step Time-Based Volume (TBV) strategy. Designed to isolate institutional absorption blocks inside active Fair Value Gaps, this approach features advanced entry models using both 50% equilibrium retracements and opening price retests to maximize risk-to-reward ratios.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Step 1: Fair Value Gap Identification (The Core Filter)

Isolate clear institutional imbalances on your charts to define high-probability interest zones.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Focus on the 5-Minute, 15-Minute, or 1-Hour charts for optimal results.

Key Levels to Watch: Locate a valid 3-candle Fair Value Gap (FVG) and clearly highlight its outer structural boundaries.

Step 2: Step 2: Swing Point Mapping

Locate a key retail liquidity pool engineered near or within your higher timeframe imbalance zone.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Same-timeframe tracking chart.

Key Levels to Watch: Identify a structural swing low (for long setups) or a swing high (for short setups) resting near or directly inside the highlighted FVG area.

Step 3: Step 3: Single-Candle Sweep and Close Validation

Validate institutional order absorption by tracking how price behaves as it sweeps your key levels.

Specific Conditions & Timeframes: Same-timeframe chart. Watch the very first candle that expands past the swing line.

Key Levels to Watch: The horizontal liquidity sweep line.

Live Example: In a long setup, the very first candle to sweep the swing low must touch or trade within the FVG boundaries. Crucially, it must reverse aggressively and close as a strong bullish candle (closing above its own open). This single-candle signature confirms that retail sellers were completely absorbed by institutional buy limits.

Step 4: Step 4: Advanced Entry Tooling Selection

Apply advanced entry techniques to optimize your entry positioning and enhance your risk-to-reward ratio.

Technique A (Aggressive Entry): Execute a market entry the exact second the validated absorption candle closes.

Technique B (Precise 50% Equilibrium Entry): Anchor a Fibonacci tool from the low to the high of the sweeping candle. Enable the 0.5 Equilibrium level and place a limit order exactly at this 50% mid-point to catch a deeper retracement.

Technique C (Opening Price Retest Entry): Position a limit order directly at the horizontal Opening Price line of the sweeping candle, anticipating a precise retest before expansion.

Key Rules & Conditions

The First-Candle Exclusivity Rule: The absorption setup must be delivered by the very first candle that sweeps the liquidity line. If the sweeping candle closes bearish on a long setup, the entire trade is immediately invalidated.

The FVG Boundary Invalidation Rule: A valid FVG consists of 3 distinct candles (1, 2, 3). If the sweeping candle drops completely below the absolute low of candle 1, the FVG structure is broken, and the setup is voided.

The Higher Timeframe Draw Confluence: While this model can be traded mechanically on its own, aligning your trades with macro targets (such as session highs/lows or previous day highs/lows) significantly boosts setup reliability.

Risk Management

The 50% Entry Trade-Off Rule: Utilizing the 50% equilibrium limit technique significantly improves your risk-to-reward ratio (often hitting 1:5 RR), but it will cause you to miss certain fast-moving setups that do not pull back. Trade sizes should be adjusted to balance this trade-off.

Protective stops must be placed cleanly past the absolute high or low of the sweeping candle body/wick structure to give the trade enough room to breathe.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The 3-Step TBV model uses pure candle-closing logic to systematically confirm institutional order tracking.

Blending Fibonacci standard deviations with internal target pools allows traders to map highly accurate exits while maintaining an exceptional risk-to-reward profile.

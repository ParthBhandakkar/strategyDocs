4. Stupid ICT IFVG Trading Strategy That Works

Source: Faiz SMC ("Stupid ICT IFVG Trading Strategy That Works")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5nhJEC59k4

Overview

This video introduces the "Inversion Fair Value Gap" (IFVG) strategy. It focuses on using SMT divergence as an added confluence, specifically for trading NQ and ES.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Bias Identification

Check daily timeframe. If unclear, use the 4-hour timeframe for swing lows/highs.

Identify SMT divergence (e.g., NQ makes a new low, ES fails to).

Step 2: Trade Execution

Once price taps into a 15-minute key level/FVG, drop to 1-minute (up to 5-minute).

Check 1, 2, 3, 4, 5-minute timeframes for the highest timeframe IFVG.

Enter on inversion of the IFVG.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade within 09:30 AM – 11:30 AM NY time.

SMT is optional but improves win rate.

Setup is invalid if the nearest liquidity is hit before the trade enters.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset: NQ

Entry: 2-minute FVG inversion, 4-hour SMT.

Outcome: W (1:1.5 RRR).

Risk Management

1:1 or 0.7 for partials.

1:1.5 or 1:2 for full TP.

Break even immediately after closest liquidity is taken.

Summary / Key Takeaways

IFVG is one of the strongest PDAs.

Protect capital first, profit second.

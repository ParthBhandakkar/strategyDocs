Finding The Correct Draw On Liquidity

Source: Faiz SMC ("Finding The Correct Draw On Liquidity With 96% Accuracy..")
Video URL: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vT_xPTnsZ5s

Overview

This video reveals a "secret technique" to identify the correct "draw on liquidity" with 96% accuracy, utilizing the 0.79 Fibonacci retracement level on the 1-minute timeframe within a 15-minute range to determine direction.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Define Session Range

Mark the high and low of the session (e.g., London session) on the 15-minute timeframe.

Wait for a sweep of either the session high or session low.

Step 2: Apply Fibonacci Tool

Apply the Fibonacci retracement tool from the session low to the high (if high was swept) or vice-versa.

Add and enable the 0.79 level.

Step 3: Signal and Entry

On the 1-minute timeframe, wait for a candle to close below/above the 0.79 level.

This closure confirms the bias towards the opposite session liquidity point (e.g., if London high was swept, price is drawn to London low).

Identify an extreme FVG within the dealing range for entry.

Key Rules & Conditions

The session high/low must be taken before the New York session open.

If no setup occurs before New York, prioritize the London session's high/low.

If price fails to touch/close beyond the 0.79 level, avoid the trade.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: Futures (MNQ)

Session: London low swept, London high targeted.

Entry: 50% of the FVG within the dealing range.

Outcome: TP hit, RR > 1:1.5.

Risk Management

As soon as the trade reaches a 1:1.5 risk-to-reward ratio, move the stop to break even.

Do not move stops prematurely.

Use the FVG as the entry point with the stop loss below the low of the dealing range.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The 0.79 Fib level is the "sweet spot" for direction.

This strategy works on Forex, Crypto, and Futures.

Discipline in following the mechanical 15-minute range and 1-minute signal is key.

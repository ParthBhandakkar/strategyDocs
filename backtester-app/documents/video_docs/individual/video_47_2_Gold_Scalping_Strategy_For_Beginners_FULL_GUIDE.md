Overview

A simplified gold scalping guide focusing on 1-hour key levels (Highs/Lows and FVGs) and 5-minute inversion models. It emphasizes a strict limit of 3 trades per day (Asia, London, New York) and highlights specific risk management rules for gold.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Key Levels (1-Hour Timeframe)

Mark highs and lows and all 1-hour FVGs.

Step 2: Entry Model (5-Minute Timeframe)

Wait for price to tap into the 1-hour key level/FVG.

Drop to 5-minute timeframe. Identify dealing range high/low.

Identify all 5-minute FVGs within this range.

Wait for price to close above/below all identified FVGs (inversion).

Step 3: Execution

Entry on candle closure.

SL below the candle body.

Break Even: As soon as price hits the nearest liquidity pool (nearest high/low).

Key Rules & Conditions

Max 3 trades/day: 1 during Asia, 1 London, 1 New York.

If the first trade is a win, stop trading for the day.

If price takes out the break-even zone before the entry closes, the setup is invalid.

Avoid trading before high-impact news.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Entry: 5-minute inversion setup.

Stop Loss: Below candle bodies.

Outcome: W (1:2 RRR).

Example 2:

Entry: 5-minute FVG inversion (New York session).

Stop Loss: Above candle body.

Outcome: W (1:2 RRR).

Risk Management

Move to break even at the nearest liquidity level.

Take 30% partial at 1:1.

Hold remainder to 1:2 RRR or nearest liquidity pool.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Use 1-hour levels for bias.

5-minute inversions are the trigger.

Protect capital by moving stop to break-even aggressively.

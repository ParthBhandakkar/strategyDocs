Liquidity Range Trading Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("The Only Liquidity Strategy You'll Ever Need")
Video URL: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LivWGyobZcA

Overview

This video introduces a range-based trading strategy on the 5-minute timeframe. It focuses on identifying a range high and low (defined by a market structure shift within a pullback) and trading the sweep of these levels.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Range

Determine the main trend direction (e.g., bullish).

Identify the big push (range high).

Wait for a pullback and a market structure shift within that pullback to define the range low.

Step 2: Execute Trade

Wait for a sweep of the range high or low.

Look for a 5-minute market structure shift and a price closure back inside the range.

Enter via an auto block or breaker block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Timeframes: 5-minute or higher.

Timezone: New York.

Only trade 3 trades max per session (Asia, London, New York).

If the setup is invalid, skip to the next.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset: NQ

Entry: From an auto block after a sweep of range low and a return into the range.

Stop: Below the auto block.

Outcome: TP hit at range high.

Risk Management

Move to break even at 1:1.5 RR.

First partial at 1:2 RR (30–35% of position).

Second partial at 0.5% (mid-range level).

Final TP at the range high/low.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Discipline and sticking to the model are the keys to the top 1%.

Mechanical identification of ranges is the core requirement.

Do not trade if the range does not exist or if the setup is not clean.

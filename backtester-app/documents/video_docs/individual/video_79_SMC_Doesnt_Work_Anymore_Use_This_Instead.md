Overview

This video addresses frustrations with common Smart Money Concept (SMC) strategies, arguing that price often doesn't respect standard order blocks because the move has already happened. The author introduces a refined entry pattern based on "inverse" fair value gaps to improve entry precision.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Order Flow

Timeframe: 4-hour.

Process: Within seconds of opening a chart, identify the market trend. If unclear, skip the pair.

Step 2: Mark Liquidity and Dealing Range

Process: Mark significant highs/lows. Identify the dealing range (the most recent low to the most recent high).

Step 3: Execute via Inverse Fair Value Gaps

Process:

If there is only one fair value gap (FVG) in the dealing range, wait for price to close below/above it. Once inversed, enter immediately.

If there are multiple FVGs, wait for the extreme FVG in the dealing range to be inversed before entering.

Stop Loss: Place slightly below the inverse FVG (or the last swept low/high).

Key Rules & Conditions

Only use the 4-hour timeframe.

The setup requires a sweep of liquidity in the opposite direction of the order flow.

Trading with the trend is mandatory.

Risk Management

Closest liquidity sweep = break even point.

Take a 35-40% partial at 1:1 reward-to-risk.

Exit full position at 2x-2.5x RR.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Don't wait for a market structure shift if the FVG inverse entry is available; taking the retest entry can lead to better outcomes.

Trading on the 4-hour timeframe provides cleaner price action than lower timeframes.

Stop loss placement requires practice to keep it tight but safe.

Overview

This execution model provides an optimized, standalone blueprint specifically tuned for Gold (XAU/USD) and Nasdaq (NQ) futures. Operating on the core principle that nearly 98% of all 1-hour candles form structural tail wicks during their initial development, this approach maps the pre-open 5-minute liquidity parameters to find high-probability scalping entries right inside these candle wicks.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Hour Open Identification

Timeframe: 1-hour chart (1H).

Action: Isolate the candle of interest at the start of the hour and draw a clean horizontal line marking its exact opening value across the screen.

Step 2: 5-Minute Range Boundary Identification

Timeframe: Shift directly down to the 5-minute chart (5M).

Action: Look to the immediate left of the vertical opening period line. Locate the most recent un-swept 5-minute swing high point and the most recent un-swept 5-minute swing low point. Mark these as your core liquidity levels.

Step 3: 1-Minute Liquidity Sweep & Projection Alignment

Timeframe: Move down to the 1-minute execution chart (1M).

Action: Wait for the price to break outside the 5-minute boundaries to hunt for liquidity.

Validation Tool: Map the manipulation extension by drawing a Fibonacci channel over the most prominent market structure shift that formed right before the session open. Draw it from the body of that structural high candle to the body of the low candle. The sweep low/high should ideally test the -2.0 to -2.5 standard deviation zone.

Step 4: Order Entry Trigger

Action: Once the manipulation target is hit, wait for a 1-minute market structure shift (MSS) or a solid Change in State of Delivery (CISD) line break. Enter instantly at the market or via a limit order on the first retest of the resulting FVG, breaker block, or volume imbalance.

Key Rules & Conditions

Rule 1: Limit trade execution to a maximum of 3 highly filtered trades per day (strictly one per major global session: Asia, London, New York).

Rule 2: The underlying market manipulation moves must take place below the hourly open line for long setups (Discount) and above the hourly open line for short positions (Premium).

Rule 3: Because Gold is highly volatile, price action will often surge away from your entry zone without a deep pullback. If the entry setup presents an optimized CISD shift, taking a direct market entry with a structural stop is valid.

Rule 4: If a fresh candle opens before your profit target is reached, immediately trail your stop loss to breakeven or close out the trade to clear time-window risk.

Risk Management

Always structure your stop losses around absolute structural highs or lows.

If a large market wick distorts the chart layout, place your Fibonacci anchor coordinates right at the candle bodies to prevent skewed target calculations.

Always accept a lower fixed profit target (such as a 1:2 ratio) when standard deviation targets stretch too far outside the average hourly volume range.

Overview

A detailed 1-minute scalping strategy for gold (and indices) based on creating a valid range, identifying a range low and high, and trading the breakout/retest.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Define the Range

Wait for price to push, then pull back.

Range Low: Identify a low and a subsequent market structure shift (break of structural high/low).

Range High: Validate by ensuring price reaches at least the 0.5 (50%) level of the range.

Step 2: Trading the Range

Wait for price to sweep either the Range High or Range Low.

Wait for a market structure shift (1-minute timeframe).

Ensure price closes back within the range.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only trade during active sessions (Asia, London, New York).

Avoid trading during high-impact news.

Valid range requires 0.5 level retracement.

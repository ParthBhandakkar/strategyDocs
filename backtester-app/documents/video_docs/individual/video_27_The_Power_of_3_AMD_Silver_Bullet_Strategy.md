Key Rules & Conditions

The -2.0 Threshold Boundary Rule: If price runs directly into the -2.0 standard deviation target level before pulling back to hit the entry PD array, the setup is deemed instantly invalid. Price is over-extended and vulnerable to a reversal.

The Negative 1.5 Second Silver Bullet Alternative: If you miss the initial entry confirmation, look for a secondary structural close past the -1.5 deviation level. If it closes cleanly above/below this line without hitting -2.0, you can look for aggressive entries at the nearest local volume imbalance.

Minimum Target Rule: The trade setup must deliver a math-proven minimum of 2.0 Risk-to-Reward (2 RR) directly matching the -2.0 standard deviation line. If it does not, the setup is discarded.

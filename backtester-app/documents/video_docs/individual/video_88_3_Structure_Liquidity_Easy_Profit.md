3. Structure + Liquidity = Easy Profit

Source: Faiz SMC ("Structure + Liquidity = Easy Profit")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NF26IyivVY

Overview

This video explains how to trade with the trend by combining structure and liquidity. It introduces the concepts of internal and external range liquidity and provides a 3-timeframe alignment method.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Order Flow/Structure

Determine if the market is trending (higher highs/lows or lower highs/lows) or ranging. Only trade with the trend.

Step 2: Utilize Liquidity

External Range Liquidity: Highs and lows.

Internal Range Liquidity: Fair Value Gaps in the discount/premium zone.

Step 3: 3-Timeframe Alignment

4-hour for structure.

15-minute for market structure shift and entry.

1-minute for final entry refinement (if needed).

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade with the trend, never against it.

Wait for price to enter a discount/premium zone before considering an entry.

Use SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence for confirmation.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: Euro AUD (4-hour)

Setup: Bullish structure, price enters a discount fair value gap, 15-minute market structure shift, 1-minute entry confirmation.

Example 2:

Asset/Pair: NZD/USD (15-minute)

Setup: Bearish trend, price enters a fair value gap, SMT divergence confirmed, entry taken after change in state of delivery.

Risk Management

Stop loss placed just beyond the structural point (e.g., auto block).

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading with the trend significantly increases probability.

Understand the difference between internal and external liquidity.

Use SMT divergence as a confirmation tool.

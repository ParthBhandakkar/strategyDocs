Overview

This video presents a highly mechanical, single-timeframe scalping strategy developed for Gold (XAUUSD) using the Fixed Range Volume Profile on a 5-minute chart. It details how to interpret volume profile geometric shapes (D, P, and B shapes) to trade failed auction reversals at value extremes or ride high-velocity momentum breakouts.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Configure Volume Profile and Chart Settings

Asset & Timeframe: Gold (XAUUSD) mapped strictly on the 5-minute timeframe. Avoid looking at the 1-minute or 15-minute charts.

Tool Setup: Select the Fixed Range Volume Profile tool and set the row size parameter to 1000 for maximum structural precision. Enable the Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and Point of Control (POC) under style settings.

Core Definitions:

Value Area (VA): The central structural block between VAH and VAL containing roughly 70% of the session's transacted volume. Represents balanced, fair value.

Point of Control (POC): The horizontal red line marking the single price level where the absolute highest concentration of volume was transacted; acts as a heavy price magnet and consolidation hub.

Discovery Mode: Price trading outside the VAH/VAL extremes, actively searching for new value.

Step 2: Plot the Precision Session Profile Boundaries

Timezone Settings: Ensure your trading platform clock is set to New York time.

Drawing Rule: Every single trading day, draw the Fixed Range Volume Profile across the London session, starting exactly from 03:00 AM New York time until 07:00 AM New York time. Ensure the data settings are locked to "manual" so the lines remain fixed when adjusting the chart view.

Step 3: Analyze the Volume Profile Shape Geometry

D-Shape Geometry: Volume is concentrated symmetrically in the middle of the range. Indicates a balanced, consolidating session. This provides the ideal conditions for Failed Auction Setups (fading the edges).

P-Shape Geometry: Volume is heavily concentrated at the top of the range. Indicates that buyers are in control and positioning for an upside breakout setup.

B-Shape Geometry: Volume is heavily concentrated at the bottom of the range. Indicates that sellers are positioning for a downside breakout setup.

Step 4: Execute the Failed Auction Setup (Edge Fading)

Long Failed Auction Rule: Price is trading above the London session VAL line. A 5-minute candle must push down and print a full close below the VAL. The immediate subsequent candles must reverse and print a solid close back inside the value area (reclaim above VAL). Enter long on the reclaim close or on a touch retest of the VAL line, place the stop loss below the sweep low, and target the POC or the opposite VAH boundary.

Short Failed Auction Rule: A 5-minute candle pushes up and prints a full close above the VAH line. The market must immediately reject and print a solid close back below the VAH line. Enter short on the reclaim close or on a touch retest of the VAH line, place the stop loss above the sweep high, and target the POC or the opposite VAL boundary.

Pre-Closed Exception: If price is already trading entirely below the VAL or above the VAH at the exact 07:00 AM session conclusion, you do not need a fresh breakout close; you simply enter on the first valid reclaim close back inside the boundary.

Step 5: Execute the Structural Breakout Setup

Bullish Breakout Rule (P-Shape Preferred): Price closes above the VAH line. Instead of an instant reclaim close, price holds its ground and builds a new horizontal fractal balance (consolidates above VAH for a prolonged period). Wait for a 5-minute candle to break above the high of this new consolidation pattern. Enter long on the breakout or its immediate retest, place the stop loss below the consolidation block, and target a fixed 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.

Bearish Breakout Rule (B-Shape Preferred): Price closes below the VAL and establishes a consolidation block underneath it for an extended period. Execute short once a candle breaks below the consolidation lows. Enter directly or on the retest, place the stop loss above the consolidation block, and target a fixed 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.

Key Rules & Conditions

The POC Invalidation Rule: If the price trades into and touches the horizontal red Point of Control (POC) line before your entry setup triggers, the trade is immediately canceled and invalid.

Daily Max Trade Limit: A maximum of 2 trades are permitted per day, strictly limited to 1 trade per direction (e.g., if you take a long trade, you cannot take another long trade that day, regardless of the outcome).

Discretionary Context: High volume at the bottom of a B-shape or top of a P-shape can occasionally represent institutional position offloading rather than a trend; always let the breakout of the consolidation confirm the direction.

Risk Management

The POC Reversal Management: Because the Point of Control (POC) represents the highest concentration of historical volume, it acts as an extreme reversal hazard. You must either take full profits, secure substantial partials, or adjust your stop loss to break-even the exact moment price tags the POC line.

Capital Defense: Protect your account aggressively. If a trade hits a 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio and the structural room to the target is narrow, adjust the stop loss to break-even immediately to eliminate downside risk.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Limiting your chart view to a single 5-minute timeframe on a highly liquid asset like Gold filters out intraday market noise and provides clear structural setups.

The shape of the volume profile (D, P, or B) dictates whether you should focus on fading session extremes or preparing to ride breakout momentum.

Patience is mandatory; you must wait for definitive 5-minute candle closures to confirm reclaims or breakouts rather than executing prematurely on wicks.

Overview

This video describes a session-based trading strategy specifically for Gold, focusing on the Asian, London, and New York sessions. It uses 5-minute charts to identify ranges based on structure shifts and targets liquidity sweeps.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Range Identification

Look for a strong push in any direction during the 5-minute session.

Wait for a pullback and a market structure shift within that pullback to define the range.

Step 2: Trade Execution

Await a sweep of the range high or low.

Confirm with a 5-minute market structure shift and a closure back inside the range.

Enter at an auto block (consecutive down/up close candles that swept liquidity).

Key Rules & Conditions

3 trades max (one per session).

Trading happens on the 5-minute timeframe.

The entry auto block is the primary location for trade activation.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset: Gold

Entry: Auto block after a range low sweep and market structure shift.

Outcome: TP hit.

Risk Management

Use Fib tool from range low to range high.

Take first partial at 0.5% of the range after entry.

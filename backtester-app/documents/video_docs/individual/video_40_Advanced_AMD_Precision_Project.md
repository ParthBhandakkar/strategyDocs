Overview

This strategy refines the core ICT Power of Three (PO3) model by mapping out precise mathematical targets for both market manipulation and distribution phases. By using standard deviation projections alongside a mechanical daily bias, this approach removes the guesswork from trading institutional accumulation, manipulation, and distribution (AMD) cycles.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Determine Daily Directional Bias

Timeframes Used: Daily (1D) chart.

Key Conditions to Confirm Trend Bias: Use the two-candle technique to define your bias.

Bullish Bias: Confirmed when the second daily candle closes above the high of the first daily candle.

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) Bearish Bias: Occurs when the second daily candle wicks above the first day's high but reverses to close bearish. This sets a short bias for the third day, with profit targets projected down to the 0.79 OTE Fibonacci level calculated from the macro swing.

Step 2: Use Standard Deviation to Project the Manipulation Phase

Timeframes Used: 15-Minute (15M) chart.

Key Levels to Watch: Identify the daily opening price and locate the sellside liquidity pools plotted to its left.

Fibonacci Mathematical Setup: Apply the Fibonacci tool to the most recent 15M structural swing (from swing high to swing low) before the daily candle open. Modify your settings to include the -2.0 and -2.5 standard deviation extension levels. The daily manipulation wick will frequently end precisely within this -2.0 to -2.5 target zone.

Step 3: Identify the Reversal Signature and Project the Expansion Target

Timeframes Used: 15-Minute (15M) chart.

Execution Triggers: Once price hits the standard deviation manipulation zone and clears the liquidity pool, look for a Change in a State of Delivery (CISD) or a clear Market Structure Shift (MSS).

Expansion Target Calculation: To find your profit target, apply the Fibonacci tool in reverse—from the final manipulation low to the swing high just before the structural shift. The -2.0 extension level serves as your primary target for the daily distribution phase.

Entry Method: Enter the trade immediately upon the validation close or on a retest of the confirmed 15M fair value gap or order block.

Key Rules & Conditions

Session Restrictions: Trade entries must be executed exclusively within the London or New York session windows. Trading during the Asian session is strictly forbidden, as it typically serves as the initial accumulation phase.

Structural Flexibility: An accumulation phase does not need to look like a tight, choppy consolidation range; it can form as a broader, sloping structure before the manipulation move takes place.

Overview

This video provides a breakdown of a successful trade on GBPUSD, demonstrating how identifying order flow on higher timeframes and using lower-timeframe confirmations leads to high RR trades.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Higher Timeframe Analysis (1 Hour)

Identify the order flow direction on the 1-hour chart. The market should have clean, obvious order flow.

Step 2: 1-Hour FVG Identification

Mark out a 1-hour FVG that aligns with the established order flow.

Step 3: 15-Minute SMT Divergence

Wait for price to tap into the 1-hour FVG and then drop to the 15-minute timeframe to look for SMT divergence between correlating pairs (e.g., GBPUSD and EURUSD).

Step 4: Change in State of Delivery (CSOD)

Look for a candle that sweeps a liquidity low, then wait for the price to close above that candle's body.

Step 5: Entry

Take entry on the retest of the formed autoblock.

Key Rules & Conditions

Ensure the pair is not in consolidation on the higher timeframe.

Trading should occur within Killzone hours.

Aim for 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: GBPUSD & EURUSD

Setup: SMT divergence between the two pairs.

Entry: Retest of the autoblock.

Outcome: Hit 1:2 RR for a large profit.

Risk Management

Stop loss is placed just below the low of the autoblock.

Strict 1:2 risk-to-reward target.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading boils down to identifying order flow, waiting for key level interaction, and executing with a clear entry model.

Do not predict; react to price action.

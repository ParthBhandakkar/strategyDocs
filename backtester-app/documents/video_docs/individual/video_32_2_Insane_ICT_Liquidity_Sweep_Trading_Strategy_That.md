Overview

This video breaks down a single-timeframe, mechanical trading model based on 15-minute candle formations following a liquidity sweep. The core edge relies on time-based volume (TBV), analyzing internal order flow absorption directly within a single candle without needing higher timeframe bias, multi-timeframe analysis, or complex trend determination.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Mark Intraday Liquidity Levels (15-Minute Chart)

Plot the key short-term structural highs and lows on a pure 15-minute timeframe:

Valid Arrays: Only use liquidity pools (swing highs/lows) engineered during the current daily cycle. The oldest allowed structural arrays must be formed after 12:00 AM midnight of the overnight session.

Proximity Factor: Prioritize immediate, closely-formed structural highs and lows. Gaps that span across extensive history or older daily cycles exhibit lower accuracy.

Step 2: Monitor for a Liquidity Sweep and Specific Candle Close

Wait for price to sweep the marked liquidity level. The strategy requires the very first candle that sweeps the liquidity to fulfill strict structural closing requirements:

Long Setup (Sell Side Sweep): The 15-minute candle must wick below the marked structural low to capture liquidity, bounce upward, and close as a bullish green candle.

Short Setup (Buy Side Sweep): The 15-minute candle must wick above the marked structural high to capture liquidity, reject downward, and close as a bearish red candle.

This dynamic forms Time-Based Volume (TBV), confirming the immediate absorption of aggressive market orders by passive limit orders at key levels.

Step 3: Direct Market Execution

As soon as the 15-minute sweeping candle closes with the correct structural color and signature, execute a position immediately at the open of the next candle using a market order:

Buy instantly if a structural low was swept and the candle closed bullish.

Sell instantly if a structural high was swept and the candle closed bearish.

No lower timeframe confirmation or structural shift is required.

Key Rules & Conditions

Session Versatility: This strategy applies across all global trading sessions (Asian, London, and New York sessions) and functions across various asset classes, including Forex, Indices, Futures, and Crypto.

Invalidation Filters:

If a 15-minute candle sweeps a structural low but closes bearish, the trade is void.

If a 15-minute candle sweeps a structural high but closes bullish, the trade is void.

The Position Reversal Rule: If you are long from a 15-minute buy setup and the very next candle moves up, sweeps the nearest structural high, and closes bearish, you must immediately close the long position at a small loss or scratch, and reverse into a short position with a stop loss placed above the new high.

Risk Management

The Break-Even Management Model: Once the position reaches a 1:1 risk-to-reward ratio, you must take a partial profit and move your stop loss to the structural break-even point.

Daily Trade Limits: Restrict execution to a maximum of 2 to 3 trades per day to prevent over-trading and revenge trading.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Waiting for the sweeping candle to close with the correct color helps filter out false breakouts and continuation runs.

Taking partials and moving stops to break-even at 1:1 ensures consistency and protects capital over time.

Single timeframe 15-minute charts provide a clean, mechanical view of liquidity sweeps without the noise of multi-timeframe analysis.

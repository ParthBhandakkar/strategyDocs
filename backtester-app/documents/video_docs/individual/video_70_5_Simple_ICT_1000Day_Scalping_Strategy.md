Overview

This video covers an intraday strategy for NQ, combining daily bias, liquidity sweeps, and price manipulation at the market open to achieve consistent results.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Daily Bias

Determine the daily bias by checking if price is in a PD array or has swept liquidity on the daily timeframe.

Step 2: Engineering Liquidity

On the 1-hour timeframe, wait for price to engineer liquidity (e.g., equal highs/lows) inside a daily PD array before the 9:30 am open.

Step 3: Liquidity Sweep (9:30 Open)

After the 9:30 am open, wait for the manipulation move to sweep the engineered liquidity.

Step 4: Entry Model

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe to look for an autoblock or inversion to enter the trade.

Key Rules & Conditions

If no liquidity is built up inside the daily PD array, avoid the setup.

Price must sweep liquidity at the 9:30 am open (Judas swing).

A change in the state of delivery (candle closure) is a key confirmation for entry.

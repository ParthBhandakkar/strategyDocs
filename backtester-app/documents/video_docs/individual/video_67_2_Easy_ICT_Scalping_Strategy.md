2. Easy ICT Scalping Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("Easy ICT Scalping Trading Strategy That Makes $1,000/Day")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9lsHH1WwKg

Overview

This video explains a 1-minute scalping strategy for NQ, emphasizing the use of high-timeframe order flow and 1-minute liquidity sweeps to enter high-probability trades.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: 15-Minute Order Flow Identification

Before the 9:30 am market open, identify the order flow on the 15-minute timeframe for NQ.

Step 2: 15-Minute FVG Tapping

Wait for price to tap into the 15-minute FVG after the 9:30 am open.

Step 3: 1-Minute SMT Divergence

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe to look for SMT divergence with ES inside the 15-minute FVG.

Step 4: Entry Model

Look for FVG inversion on the 1-minute timeframe and take the entry upon the candle close.

Key Rules & Conditions

Focus exclusively on NQ and ES.

If the market is in consolidation, avoid trading.

SMT Divergence: NQ shows strength when it fails to create a new low while ES does.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Asset/Pair: NQ/ES

Entry: FVG inversion.

Outcome: Strong movement towards the target, hitting 1:1 RR for the first partial.

Risk Management

Break even immediately after the closest high/low is taken out.

Take first partial at 1:1 RR (or 0.7 if in consolidation).

Final TP at the next equal highs/lows level.

Summary / Key Takeaways

The model relies heavily on high-timeframe order flow and 1-minute liquidity sweeps.

Consistency and following the risk management rules are essential for success.

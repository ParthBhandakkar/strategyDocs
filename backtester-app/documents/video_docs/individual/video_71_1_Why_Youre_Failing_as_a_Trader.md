1. Why You're Failing as a Trader

Source: Faiz SMC ("Please Quit Trading Before Its Too Late!")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqY4uL1a-oQ

Overview

This video addresses the psychological and behavioral reasons why most traders fail, emphasizing that success in trading is primarily a matter of internal work and self-mastery rather than just having a "good" strategy.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identifying the Root Cause

The author argues that many traders bring personal trauma and unstable emotional patterns into trading, leading to impulsive, fear-based, or greedy decisions instead of logical, systematic ones.

Step 2: Breaking Bad Habits

Stop overtrading and forcing setups due to boredom or a need for dopamine.

Stop "vibing" or guessing; create and stick to a plan.

Commit to one strategy for at least six months; stop switching strategies constantly.

Stop chasing trades after the move has already happened.

Step 3: Changing Your Identity

Shift from seeing trading as a lottery ticket to viewing it as a business. Take full accountability for losses rather than blaming mentors, market conditions, or external factors.

Key Rules & Conditions

Stop being addicted to outcomes; focus on the process.

Prioritize discipline, self-control, and patience.

Regulate your nervous system.

Treat trading as a professional endeavor.

Risk Management

Treat the trading account as a business, not a video game for broken egos.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading is a war of attrition against your own weaknesses.

The market asks every day if you have evolved.

If you cannot handle the discipline required, it is better to walk away from trading.

Overview

This video introduces an optimized, multi-timeframe approach that links 1-hour structural trends with lower-timeframe fair value gap inversions. It highlights a systematic process to identify highest-probability timeframes (from 1-minute to 5-minute charts) to maximize entry precision.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Determine the 1-Hour Trend & Draw on Liquidity

Before the New York open, analyze the 1-hour chart to establish your directional bias. Look for respected structural points:

If price respects 1-hour Bearish PD Arrays and breaks through Bullish arrays, focus exclusively on short setups and target key swing lows.

If price respects 1-hour Bullish PD Arrays, focus exclusively on long setups and target key swing highs.

Step 2: Wait for post-9:30 AM Fair Value Gap Generation

After 9:30 AM EST, monitor the 5-minute or 15-minute timeframe. Look for the first clean Fair Value Gap that forms in the direction of your 1-hour trend bias.

Step 3: Execute via the Multi-Timeframe Inversion Ladder

When price scales back to mitigate the 5m/15m Fair Value Gap, drop down to the 1-minute timeframe to locate the inversion setup. To maximize precision, use the Multi-Timeframe Inversion Ladder:

Check the 1-minute chart for a fair value gap.

Cycle through the 2-minute, 3-minute, 4-minute, and 5-minute charts to find the highest-timeframe fair value gap inside that entry leg.

The Golden Rule: Always prioritize and trade the highest-timeframe inversion gap available up to the 5-minute chart. Wait for a clean, displaced candle close to reverse past that gap to trigger your entry.

Key Rules & Conditions

The V-Shape Recovery Rule: The lower-timeframe inversion must print as a sharp, clean V-shaped return. Avoid any trades where price grinds sideways or consolidates inside the gap, as this leaves behind unmitigated liquidity pools that increase your risk.

Stop Loss Placement: Place your stop loss securely past the structural candle bodies of the inversion block.

Risk Management

The First-Low Break-Even Rule: Move your stop loss to break-even the moment price clears the very first minor swing high or low formed immediately after your entry. This strategy ensures you protect your capital and manage risk effectively.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Always cycle from 1-minute to 5-minute charts to find the highest-timeframe inversion gap available.

Prioritize sharp, clean V-shaped recoveries and avoid slow, grinding consolidations inside your target zones.

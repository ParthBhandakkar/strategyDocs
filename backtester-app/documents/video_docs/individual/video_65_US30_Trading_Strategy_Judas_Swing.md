US30 Trading Strategy (Judas Swing)

Source: Faiz SMC ("Best ICT Judas Swing Trading Strategy With 79% Winrate!")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s0jk8ENNkjw

Overview

An easy-to-learn US30 trading strategy based on identifying the most recent high and low on the 15m time frame before the 9:30 AM (New York) open, then trading the sweep of these levels. It is designed to be highly mechanical.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Pre-Open Setup

Timeframe: 15-minute.

Action: Before 9:30 AM open (New York time), mark the most recent high and low.

Step 2: The Trade

Action: Drop to 1m TF after 9:30 AM open. Wait for a sweep of the 15m high or low.

Condition: Look for a market structure shift with displacement (FVG).

Entry: Enter from the FVG or autoblock (OB). Place stop loss below the low of the sweep.

Target: Target the opposite marked 15m level.

Key Rules & Conditions

Specifically optimized for US30.

Use New York time on Trading View.

Market structure shift must have a body closure.

Must see displacement.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (US30):

Low swept, market structure shift to the upside with displacement, FVG entry, TP at the high.

Example 2 (US30):

Low swept, market structure shift to the upside, breaker block entry, TP at the high.

Risk Management

30% partial at 1:1 RR.

Move to break even at 1:1 RR.

Final TP at the opposite marked 15m level.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Highly mechanical strategy suitable for beginners.

Best results on US30.

Does not require daily bias for execution.

Employs a simple partial and break-even strategy to protect capital.

Overview

This video provides a deep dive into the Midas Model, clarifying how to identify valid highs and lows to avoid getting faked out, and breaking down recent trade examples.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)

Identifying Highs/Lows: Only use highs/lows formed after the previous day's New York session (post-12:00 PM).

The Setup:

Identify the most recent unswept high and low on the 15-minute timeframe before 8:00 PM.

After the 8:00 PM open, look for a sweep of the high or low.

Drop to the 1-minute timeframe. Wait for a "W-shaped" market structure shift (a sweep followed by an immediate recovery) that breaks structure and leaves a Fair Value Gap.

Inversion (Tricky Situations): In cases where the market structure shift is far from the price action, mark all Fair Value Gaps in the dealing range and wait for price to inverse them before entering.

Key Rules & Conditions

Highs and lows from the previous day's New York session are not valid.

You can only take the 9:00 PM trade if you are not already in an 8:00 PM trade.

If in an 8:00 PM trade that hasn't hit TP, do not take the 9:00 PM trade.

The 9:00 PM trade must be entered before the midnight open (12:00 AM).

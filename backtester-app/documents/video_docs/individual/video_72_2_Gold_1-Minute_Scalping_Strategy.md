2. Gold 1-Minute Scalping Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("GOLD 1 Minute Trading Strategy That Actually Works..")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EpmNNATDxyg

Overview

This video outlines a mechanical, 1-minute scalping strategy specifically for Gold, focusing on liquidity sweeps, market structure shifts, and fair value gaps.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Setup and Identification

Set time zone to New York (UTC-4).

Identify the most recent high and low on the 15-minute timeframe before the 8:00 PM (Asian session) open.

Step 2: Executing the Trade

Wait for price to sweep either the recent high or low.

Look for a Market Structure Shift (MSS) with displacement (a break of structure with a Fair Value Gap (FVG) present below the shift level).

Take entry from the FVG or a Breaker Block.

Step 3: Exit Strategy

Target the most recent high or low, or aim for a 1:3 reward-to-risk ratio.

Key Rules & Conditions

Order flow on the 4-hour timeframe can be used to improve win rates (look for premium/discount levels).

If the draw on liquidity is hit before entry, the setup's probability decreases.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (Gold 15m):

9:45 AM. Price sweeps recent high. MSS with displacement. Entry from a breaker block. Target 1:3.

Example 2 (Gold 1m):

9:59 AM. Price sweeps recent low. MSS with FVG and breaker block entry.

Example 3 (Gold 1m):

9:50 AM. Price sweeps recent high. MSS with body closure and breaker block entry.

Risk Management

Go break-even at 1:1.5.

Take partial profits at 1:2.

Summary / Key Takeaways

This is a highly mechanical model (dubbed the "Midas Model").

It is designed to capture high-momentum moves after a liquidity sweep.

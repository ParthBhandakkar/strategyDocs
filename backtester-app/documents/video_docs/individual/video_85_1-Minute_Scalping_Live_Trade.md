Strategy/Topic Name: 1-Minute Scalping (Live Trade)

Source: Faiz SMC ("How I Made $2,130 In 3 Minutes Day Trading")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ksLTkMjm7-Y

Overview

A short recap of a profitable 3-minute live trade, focusing on building a "story" and using SMT divergence as a confirmation tool.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Story Building (Daily/1-Hour/5-Minute)

Look at the daily chart to identify major liquidity sweeps and fair value gaps.

Use 1-hour for market structure shift (MSS).

Use 5-minute for identifying the draw on liquidity (DOL) – in this case, equal lows.

Step 2: Execution (1-Minute)

Mark the 9:30 AM opening price.

Look for an opposite liquidity sweep.

Use SMT divergence (e.g., between NQ and E-mini S&P) for confirmation.

Wait for an inverse FVG to trigger entry.

Key Rules & Conditions

The trade must occur post-9:30 AM.

Use SMT divergence to filter high-probability setups.

Wait for FVG inversion.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1:

Entry: Long position on NQ based on a 1-minute inverse FVG after an opposite liquidity sweep.

Outcome: $2,130 profit in 3 minutes.

Risk Management

Break-even at 1:1, close 40% (similar to the previous framework).

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trading context ("story") is more important than mechanical entry rules.

Do not fear election or high-news days; often, these offer the cleanest technical setups.

Use SMT as a secondary confirmation factor for high-confidence entries.

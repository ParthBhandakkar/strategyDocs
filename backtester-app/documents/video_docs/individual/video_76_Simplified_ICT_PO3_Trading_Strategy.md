Simplified ICT PO3 Trading Strategy

Source: Faiz SMC ("I Simplified ICT PO3 Trading Strategy...")
Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HT49YvYH9NQ

Overview

This video presents a simplified version of the ICT Power of 3 trading strategy, which consists of three phases: Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution. The author emphasizes the importance of identifying daily bias before executing trades to avoid getting "faked out."

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Daily Bias

Timeframe: Daily.

Process: Look at the last two daily candles and identify their highs and lows.

Rules:

If the second candle closes above the high of the first candle, the bias for the third candle is bullish.

If the second candle wicks above the first candle's high but closes below it, the bias for the third candle is bearish.

Step 2: Identify Manipulation and Expansion

Timeframe: 15-minute.

Process: Mark the opening price of the third day. Wait for a manipulation move (a wick) to the opposite side of the expected direction that sweeps significant liquidity (e.g., significant highs/lows or relative equal highs/lows).

Tool: A Fibonacci tool can be used from the last high to the last low before the market structure shift to identify valid manipulation, which usually ends around -2 and -2.5 standard deviations.

Step 3: Execution

Process: Once a valid manipulation occurs, look for a market structure shift or a break-of-structure entry from a fair value gap.

Entry: Enter at the fair value gap.

Risk Management: Target a 1:2 to 1:3 reward-to-risk ratio.

Key Rules & Conditions

Identify the daily bias before trading.

Ensure the manipulation move sweeps significant liquidity on the 15-minute timeframe.

Not every manipulation move will lead to a valid expansion.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (NQ):

Bias: Bullish (based on the second day wicking below the low of the first and closing back above).

Manipulation: Sweep of relative equal lows to the left of the opening price, reaching -2 standard deviation.

Entry: Market structure shift followed by an entry at a fair value gap.

Example 2 (NQ):

Bias: Bullish (second candle closed above the first).

Manipulation: Sweep of relative equal lows to the left of the opening price.

Entry: Order block.

Example 3 (GBPUSD):

Bias: Bearish (second candle wicks above the first and closes below).

Manipulation: Sweep of relative equal lows.

Entry: Order block following sharp displacement.

Risk Management

Target a 1:2 to 1:3 reward-to-risk ratio.

Don't hold trades for too long.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Bias identification is crucial.

The strategy relies on identifying valid manipulation moves that sweep liquidity.

Use lower timeframes for precise entries based on market structure shifts.

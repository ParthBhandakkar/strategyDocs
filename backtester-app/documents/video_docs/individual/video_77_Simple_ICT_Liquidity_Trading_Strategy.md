Simple ICT Liquidity Trading Strategy

Video URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zqw2tDMGqqA

Overview

This video introduces a liquidity trading strategy based on identifying swing highs and lows to determine daily/intraday bias, with the goal of achieving high-accuracy entries.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Identify Swing Highs and Lows

Timeframe: 4-hour.

Process: Identify swing highs (three candles where the second is highest) and swing lows (three candles where the second is lowest).

Focus: Focus specifically on the third candle of the formation.

Step 2: Mark Liquidity Levels

Process: Mark the high and low of the third candle. These levels serve as liquidity pools.

Step 3: Identify Sweep and Execute

Timeframe: 5-minute (on the next 4-hour candle).

Process: Wait for price to sweep either the high or low of the third candle.

Confirmation: Look for a market structure shift with a body closure on the 5-minute timeframe.

Entry: Enter from an order block or fair value gap.

Stop Loss: Place above the high (if selling) or below the low (if buying) of the sweep.

Key Rules & Conditions

Setup is invalid if the sweep does not occur within the next 4-hour candle.

Setup is invalid if there is no market structure shift within the 4-hour candle window.

Best win rate occurs during "killzone" hours.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (NQ):

Setup: Swing low found on 4-hour chart.

Sweep: Price sweeps the high of the third candle.

Entry: 50% of the order block on the 5-minute chart.

Outcome: 1:10 reward-to-risk ratio.

Example 2 (GBPJPY):

Setup: Swing low on 4-hour chart.

Sweep: Price sweeps the high of the third candle.

Entry: Fair value gap on 5-minute chart.

Outcome: Over 1:2 reward-to-risk ratio.

Example 3 (GBPCAD):

Setup: Swing low on 4-hour chart.

Sweep: Price sweeps the low of the third candle.

Entry: Order block.

Outcome: 1:2 reward-to-risk ratio.

Risk Management

Go break even at 1:1 reward-to-risk.

Take partial profits at 1:2.

Trail the stop loss or hold until liquidity is hit.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trade during killzone hours for a higher win rate.

If unsure about the market direction (order flow) within a few seconds of opening a chart, skip that pair.

The strategy is applicable to all pairs, assets, and crypto.

Overview

This video details a trading model focusing on higher-timeframe Points of Interest (PDAs), specifically 15-minute FVGs, combined with 1-minute SMT divergences and "W-shape" inversions for trade entry.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Mark HTF PDA

Before 9:30 AM New York time, identify 15-minute FVGs.

Step 2: Confirm Entry with LFT

Wait for price to tap into the 15-minute FVG.

On the 1-minute timeframe, check for an SMT divergence (e.g., MNQ sweeps a low, but ES fails to do so).

Confirm with a "W-shape" inversion: consecutive candles that break back through the 1-minute FVG with minimal consolidation.

Step 3: Execution

Enter at the inversion of the 1-minute FVG.

Place stop loss below the candle bodies.

Key Rules & Conditions

Trade only MNQ, use ES for SMT confirmation.

If the closest liquidity is hit before the inversion, the setup is invalid.

Avoid setups with significant consolidation; prefer sharp, "W-shape" movements.

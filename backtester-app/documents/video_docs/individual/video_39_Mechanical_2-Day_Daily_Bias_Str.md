Overview

This strategy introduces a purely mechanical method to identify daily market bias using only the price action of the last two days. By removing subjective analysis, this approach establishes a clear directional stance for the third day, using lower-timeframe sweeps to execute trades with high risk-to-reward ratios.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Analyze the Two-Day Candle Pattern on the Daily Chart

Timeframes Used: Daily (1D) chart.

Key Conditions to Confirm Trend Bias:

Bullish Bias Selection: Look for two consecutive bullish candles where the second daily candle closes fully above the high of the first daily candle. This structure sets a bullish bias for the third day.

Bearish Bias Selection: Look for two consecutive bearish candles where the second daily candle closes fully below the low of the first daily candle. This structure sets a bearish bias for the third day.

No Bias Condition: If a day prints an alternating pattern (e.g., a bearish day followed by a bullish day), there is no bias and you must stand aside.

Step 2: Mark the Daily Open and Locate Pre-Open Liquidity Pools

Timeframes Used: Transition to the 15-Minute (15M) chart once the third day opens.

Key Levels to Watch: Mark the exact opening price of the third day candle. Look to the left side of this opening line to find structural liquidity pools (such as equal lows, clean session swing lows, or prior highs) formed before the day began.

Step 3: Wait for a Liquidity Sweep and a Lower-Timeframe Structural Shift

Timeframes Used: 15-Minute (15M) chart.

Execution Triggers: Wait for price to move counter to your daily bias and sweep the pre-open liquidity pool. Once the sweep is complete, monitor the 15M chart for one of two entry signals:

Market Structure Shift (MSS): A clean structural break confirmed by a candle body closing past the recent swing high/low.

Change in a State of Delivery (CISD): A candle closing past the body of the candle sequence that ran the liquidity.

Entry Method: Enter a trade immediately on the validating 15M candle close, or place a limit order within the newly formed breaker block or fair value gap.

Key Rules & Conditions

Session Focus: This strategy is optimized for Gold and NQ markets, with execution restricted to the Asia, London, or New York session windows.

Targeting Rules: If the previous day's high or low remains unswept, use it as your primary profit target. If that level has already been cleared, default to a mechanical 1:2 or 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio.

Risk Management

Fixed Discipline: Do not manually adjust your stop loss or panic during a trade's drawdown if your structural invalidation level remains untouched. Let the trade's probabilities play out mechanically.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Comparing the price action of the last two daily candles provides a reliable, mechanical way to determine daily market bias.

Intraday trends often begin with a counter-trend manipulation move that sweeps liquidity before expanding in the true direction of the day.

Targeting the prior day's unreached highs or lows can help secure high risk-to-reward ratios.

Overview

This entry model is designed for trend continuation. It teaches how to enter trades when trading with the trend, specifically avoiding getting "faked out" by market structure. It utilizes the "Continuation Purge" concept, where price sweeps specific liquidity before continuing its trend.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Market Identification

Goal: Identify the direction of the market within 3 seconds of opening the pair.

Condition: Trade only when the trend is 100% clear (bullish or bearish). If unsure, move to another pair.

Step 2: The Continuation Purge Entry

Setup: Identify the low (if bullish trend) or high (if bearish trend) that caused the most recent break of structure.

Liquidity Sweep: Wait for price to sweep that specific low/high.

Inversion: Within the dealing range, identify fair value gaps (FVG). Wait for price to close above/below these FVGs (inversion).

Entry: Enter long/short directly after the inversion, with stops placed just below/above the bodies of the candle.

Key Rules & Conditions

Timezone: Trade during Kill Zone hours if using timeframes below 4 hours.

Break Even: Move to break even as soon as the closest liquidity pool is hit.

Invalidation: Setup is invalid if the closest liquidity pool is hit before entry.

Correlation: Use SMT divergence with a correlating asset to increase win rate.

Trade Examples From The Video
Example 1 (Gold, 5m TF):

Setup: Bullish trend, sweep of the low that caused the break of structure, FVG inversion, entry on close.

Outcome: Successful trade to the upside.

Example 2 (GBPUSD, 5m TF):

Setup: Sweep of high that caused break of structure, FVG inversion, entry on close.

Outcome: Successful trade to the downside.

Risk Management

Take 30-40% partial at 1:1 RR.

Move to break even at 1:1 RR.

Target the next major high or low for the final take profit.

Summary / Key Takeaways

Trade with the trend using the "Continuation Purge."

Wait for price to sweep the liquidity that caused the break of structure.

Use FVG inversion as the entry trigger.

Incorporate SMT divergence for higher win rates.

Overview

This video outlines a mechanical trading strategy for Gold and AUDJPY, centered around trading "Judas Swings" (liquidity sweeps) that occur after 8:00 PM and 9:00 PM (New York time). It also introduces a 5-minute time frame model for trading during the New York session based on obvious liquidity runs and fair value gap inversions.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: 8:00 PM / 9:00 PM Judas Swing Model (15m/1m TF)

Timeframe: Mark recent high/low on 15m TF before 8:00 PM or 9:00 PM open.

Action: Wait for price to sweep either the recent 15m high or low after the designated open.

Entry: Drop to 1m TF. Identify a market structure shift. Take entry and target the opposite side liquidity (the 15m level marked before open).

Note: Only one trade at a time. If 8 PM trade is taken, do not trade 9 PM.

Step 2: 5-Minute Time Frame Model

Timeframe: 5m TF only.

Action: Look for an "obvious run on liquidity" (big momentum pushing price up/down, sweeping high/low or equal highs/lows) after 8:00 PM open (for Asia) or 9:30 AM open (for New York).

Entry: Identify fair value gaps (FVG) within the dealing range created by the liquidity run. Wait for price to close below/above the FVG (inversion). Take entry on the close or retest.

Risk Management: Aim for 1:2 RR, partial at 1:1, or move to break even after hitting the closest liquidity level. Setup becomes invalid if the closest liquidity level is hit before entry.

Key Rules & Conditions

Only works for Gold and AUDJPY.

Time zone must be set to New York.

Only one trade active at a time.

If the closest liquidity level is hit before entry, the setup is invalid.

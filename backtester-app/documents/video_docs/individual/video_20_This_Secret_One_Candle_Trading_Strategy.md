Overview

This video breaks down a powerful institutional trading framework built around the 10:00 AM EST 4-hour (4H) candle. By using 1-minute chart structures along with Fibonacci standard deviation projections, this strategy identifies high-probability internal candle manipulation phases. This allows traders to capture major macro trend expansions across NQ and Gold futures.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: High Timeframe Isolation & Anchor Point Plotting

Timeframe: 4-hour chart (4H).

Asset focus: Primarily NQ (Nasdaq) and Gold futures.

Action: Isolate the 10:00 AM EST 4-hour candle exactly as it prints its opening tick. Draw a clear horizontal line across your chart to anchor this exact opening price level for reference.

Step 2: 1-Minute Initial Market Structure Mapping

Timeframe: Move directly down to the 1-minute execution chart (1M).

Action: Wait for the market to form its initial structure around the open. Identify the very first 1-minute market structure shift (MSS) that breaks cleanly either above or below your marked 10:00 AM opening price line.

Step 3: Measuring the Manipulation Phase

Action: Locate the clear fractal swing high/low sequence that formed right before that initial structural break.

Fibonacci Tool Execution: Draw a Fibonacci retracement tool across that specific pre-shift swing block. For a short manipulation trade, measure from the absolute high point to the structural swing low body.

Tool Settings Configuration: Ensure your Fibonacci extensions are set to: 0, 0.5, 1.0, -1.0, -2.0, -2.5, and -4.0.

The Target Zone: The key market manipulation phase is expected to terminate inside the standard deviation projection zone between the -2.0 and -2.5 levels.

Step 4: SMT Validation & Order Execution

Action: As price enters the calculated -2.0 to -2.5 standard deviation window, look for an SMT divergence breakout against a correlating index asset (compare NQ directly against ES).

Trigger: Wait for a 1-minute Change in State of Delivery (CISD) or a clear market structure shift to confirm the reversal. Enter on the immediate retest of the resulting FVG or breaker block.

Take Profit Projections: Draw a fresh Fibonacci tool from the low to the high of the reversal structure. Set your primary take profit target at the -2.0 projection line, or extend it to -4.0 if there is a major high-timeframe liquidity target overlapping that level.

Key Rules & Conditions

Rule 1: Long trade confirmations must form entirely below the 10:00 AM hourly open line (Discount pricing), while short entries must form entirely above the open line (Premium pricing).

Rule 2: SMT divergence must show clear visual confirmation right as price tests the key standard deviation zone, or the trade setup is invalid.

Rule 3: If the initial structural swing wick is unusually large, anchor your Fibonacci tool directly to the candle bodies instead of the wicks to prevent skewed projection targets.

Rule 4: If you miss the primary structural entry, you can take a secondary "Silver Bullet" entry by waiting for a fair value gap to form as price trends through the -1.0 and -1.5 standard deviation zone.

Always protect your open capital by trailing stops to breakeven once price moves past your initial structural targets.

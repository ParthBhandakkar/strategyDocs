Overview

This video introduces a systematic framework that uses higher-timeframe Internal Range Liquidity (IRL) pools as an institutional magnet, combined with lower-timeframe Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVG) to build a mechanical setup for high-probability setups.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Map Out Your Draw on Liquidity (DOL)

Prior to taking any trade execution, you must define the primary macro draw on liquidity. Look across higher timeframes to identify major benchmarks:

Asia Session Highs / Lows

London Session Highs / Lows

Previous Day Highs / Lows (PDH/PDL)

Significant higher timeframe equal highs or equal lows.

Step 2: Wait for New York Session Volume

The trading window opens strictly at 9:30 AM and closes at 11:30 AM New York Time. Never look for entries before 9:30 AM due to choppy price action and lower market volume.

Step 3: Identify the Higher-Timeframe Target Array

After 9:30 AM, wait for price to expand and form a new 5-minute or 15-minute Fair Value Gap (which acts as your higher timeframe Internal Range Liquidity pool) that points toward your established Draw on Liquidity.

Step 4: Monitor the Lower-Timeframe Liquidity Sweep

When price returns to touch the 5m/15m Fair Value Gap, drop down to the 1-minute chart. Verify that price executes a clear liquidity sweep of a localized 1-minute swing high or low inside the higher timeframe gap.

Step 5: Trade via the 1-Minute Inversion Trigger

Locate the bullish or bearish fair value gaps formed within that lower-timeframe leg. Wait for a 1-minute candle body to close completely past those gaps, turning them into Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVGs).

If multiple fair value gaps are present within that leg, you must wait for all of them to be cleanly inversed before entering.

Step 6: Set Execution Parameters

Entry: Open your position immediately at the close of the inversion candle.

Stop Loss: Place your stop loss past the absolute structural high or low of the setup.

Take Profit: Target a conservative 1:2 fixed risk-to-reward ratio or hold until price hits your macro Draw on Liquidity target.

Key Rules & Conditions

Displacement Requirement: The lower-timeframe inversion must print with strong displacement (large, energetic candle bodies). If the market prints small, choppy candles, invalidate the setup.

Risk Management

The Close Liquidity Break-Even Rule: Move your stop loss to break-even the moment price clears the closest internal swing high or low formed immediately after your entry. This ensures your capital is protected against sudden market turnabouts.

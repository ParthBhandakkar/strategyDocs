Overview

This highly mechanical strategy removes subjectivity by utilizing the 8:00 AM hourly candle boundaries on NQ or Gold futures to predict major pre-market and early morning expansion moves. By tracking liquidity sweeps across the high and low boundaries of this single candle alongside nearby structural swing points, traders can identify high-probability reversal execution zones.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Chart Configuration & Time Check

Confirm that your trading platform time zone is precisely set to New York time.

Navigate to the 1-hour (1H) chart for your chosen asset (NQ or Gold).

Step 2: Mark the 8:00 AM Candle Boundaries

Wait for the 8:00 AM New York hourly candle to close at exactly 09:00 AM.

Plot a horizontal line across the absolute high wick and the absolute low wick of that specific 8:00 AM candle.

Step 3: Identify Adjacent Key Swing Levels

Look closely to the immediate left on the hourly timeframe.

Identify and mark the nearest distinct structural swing high above the 8:00 AM candle high, and the nearest historical swing low below the 8:00 AM candle low. These act as your key liquidity expansion targets.

Step 4: Downshift to the 1-Minute Chart for Execution

Wait for price action to sweep past either the 8:00 AM candle high/low boundary AND its neighboring structural swing point.

Immediately downshift to the 1-minute (1M) timeframe to track real-time order flow changes.

Entry Setup Validation: Look for an explicit Market Structure Shift (MSS) or an Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG) pattern. Price must travel back across and close within the 8:00 AM candle boundary level to confirm a true liquidity trap before entering a trade.

Key Rules & Conditions

Patience Condition: Never take an execution on a breakout unless both the 8:00 AM candle boundary line and the marked structural swing highs/lows are comprehensively swept. Wicking only one of the levels increases the risk of continuity failure.

The Close Re-entry Rule: Entering a trade without price closing back inside the 8:00 AM candle level is considered highly unsafe and invalidates the setup.

SMT Divergence (Optional): Correlating NQ with ES (S&P 500) or Gold with Silver during the sweep provides significant confirmation of an upcoming structural turn.

Risk Management

The Midpoint Partial Rule: Utilize the Fibonacci Retracement tool from the 8:00 AM hourly high to low to map out the exact 50% midpoint. You should look to take partial profits or protect your entry at break-even as soon as price returns to this neutral zone.

Strict Invalidation: If price fails to hold the structural shift and trends past the external sweep low/high candle, close the trade immediately with zero discretionary extensions.

Overview

This video introduces a highly mechanical trading strategy focused on trading a single daily data block: the 10:00 AM EST 4-hour candle. It relies on the concept of Power of Three (AMD)—Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution. The primary goal is to target and execute entries within the manipulation phase (the candle's wick) to capture the distribution expansion phase on lower timeframes.

Step-by-Step Breakdown (Chronological)
Step 1: Mark the Opening Price

As soon as the 10:00 AM EST 4-hour candle opens, plot a horizontal line marking the exact opening price.

Step 2: Identify Higher Timeframe PD Arrays (A+ Setup)

Switch your charts to the 5-minute or 15-minute timeframe. Identify Premium or Discount arrays relative to the opening price:

Look for Bullish PD Arrays (Fair Value Gaps or Order Blocks) below the opening price.

Look for Bearish PD Arrays above the opening price.

Step 3: Monitor for Internal SMT Divergence

When price hits your selected 5m/15m PD Array, drop down to the 1-minute chart. You must observe a Smart Money Tool (SMT) divergence between correlating assets to confirm a market reversal:

If trading NQ, compare it with ES. For example, look for NQ making a lower low while ES fails to make a lower low.

Step 4: Confirm via Change in State of Delivery (CISD)

Wait for a clear Change in State of Delivery. This is achieved when a 1-minute candle body closes completely past the consecutive bearish candles (for a long setup) or consecutive bullish candles (for a short setup) that originally swept the liquidity leg.

Step 5: Execute & Project Targets using Fibonacci Projections

Entry: Enter at the immediate close of the CISD candle or on a brief retest of a newly formed 1-minute fair value gap.

Stop Loss: Place your stop loss just past the structural swing low/high formed by the SMT divergence leg.

Profit Target: Draw a Fibonacci expansion tool from the last structural low to the last structural high before the market structure shift occurred. Take profit targets are determined at the -2.0, -2.5, and -4.0 deviation levels. Look left to overlap these targets with obvious pools of liquidity.

Key Rules & Conditions

Asset Universe: Specifically optimized for NQ Futures and Gold Futures. Avoid using this pattern on CFD data feeds due to timing offsets.

The Core Premium/Discount Filter: Always aim to sell above the opening price (Premium) and buy below the opening price (Discount).

Setup Variations:

A+ Setup: Requires price to tap a distinct 5m or 15m PD array before confirming a lower-timeframe entry.

A Setup: If you have an established directional bias, you can look for immediate lower timeframe SMT divergence right at the open without waiting for an exact 5m/15m PD array tap.

Risk Management

Ensure stop losses are always technically validated by structural highs/lows.

Avoid forcing targets out into the -4.0 deviation if there is a massive wick size difference; instead, draw Fibonacci levels directly from candle bodies to preserve a conservative R/R layout.

Target the 10:00 AM EST 4-hour candle for daily setup setups.
